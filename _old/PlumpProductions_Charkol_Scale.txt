	===== FURAFFINITY =====


After gaining quite the hefty gut, it didn't take long for Charkol to start wondering just how large he had gotten. Considering his raw weight was unbeknown to himself, it was much to his chagrin as he set out to find a proper scale that didn't cost an arm and a leg that could properly measure his newfound heft! Even all the new and fancy smart scales that boasted to be sturdier than ever seemed to succumb to his heaviness, but not before giving him a critical error or few on his smartphone as it logged it's final measurement!
