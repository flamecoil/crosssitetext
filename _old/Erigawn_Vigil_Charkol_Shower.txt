	===== FURAFFINITY =====


When you're as large as Charkol is, a little bit of fun in the shower can very easily shift from eagerly wanting to spend a few extra minutes to get clean, to pretty much negating the whole purpose of taking the shower in the first place! It wasn't hard at all for Vigil to get Charkol riled up and prodding at his backside; the protogen's plump frame aside, the drain in the shower just meant that there'd be much less cleanup to do. Needless to say, Charkol very much took advantage of that fact!
