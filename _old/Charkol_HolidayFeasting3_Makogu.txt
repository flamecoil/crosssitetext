	===== FURAFFINITY =====


As the winter season is well on its way, Charkol's schedule of leftovers consumption has been adjusted due to friends leaving him addtiional dishes that fit his palate; meats and desserts he likes to like overfill his fridge and compell him to overfill his gut multiple times a day. Never does his belly actually request food, but each grumble and groan of his gut is more of a protest of how full it is as opposed to it simply just churning food along. Still, the charizard rarely obey's his body's slight ache as it gets used to being as full as it is. Clothes he normally sports have split or haven't been subjected to the massive width of the fire-type as he resolves to stomping about his apartment nude most of the time. 

Those who have a bashful preference for the much heavier charizard keep him company, showing up to cuddle up to him to either enjoy his overwhelming warmth or bring him yet another meal to make that belly bulge. Regardless of his guests' reasoning for being around, he oft enjoys them, and more often than not they don't mind when he ends up a little more excited than normal, what used to be a bulge contained now flaunts itself as a proud, eager arousal, often oozing wherever he manages to stomp or waddle to. Such guests he often welcomes to stay for quite some time!
