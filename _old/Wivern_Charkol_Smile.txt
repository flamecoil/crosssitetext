	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Most of the time, if you're unfamiliar with this maroon-colored charizard, you'll find him to be fairly neutral -- however respectful -- in greeting and dealing with you. In the rare moments in which he's openly amicable towards most strangers, a big grin and easy-going attitude will grant those who wish to say 'hello' a bit more time to impress the large fiery male before he becomes curt or aggressive. So, be appreciative if you're a newcomer and you're greeted with such a wide smile, and be sure you watch what you say so that open mouth doesn't suddenly deliver a flamethrower!
