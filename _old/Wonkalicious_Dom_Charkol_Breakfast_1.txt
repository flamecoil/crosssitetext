	===== FURAFFINITY =====


One should always be careful when it's revealed that someone else likes to cook, especially during a time in which you're not exactly minding your weight. Charkol was quite unaware that when Dom mentioned his affinity for cooking and serving up large meals, that the follow-up questions of favorite meals and foods could turn into a mini-buffet the next day. 

Having already let his waistline go a little bit, Charkol figured that one day of overeating wouldn't hurt all that much, and he indulged in the breakfast that Dom brought about. When about half-way through and already struggling to scarf down what was left, the chef took no time at all deciding on how to step in. Stacking pancakes, bacon, eggs and other morsels quite high, Charkol quickly was informed that refusing to eat what a chef cooked for you wasn't acceptable!
