	===== FURAFFINITY =====


[url=https://www.furaffinity.net/view/31181348/]Previously...[/url] 

Ever wanting to make the local remnants of any Halloween sweet disappear, the spirits that had decided to make Charkol's house their headquarters of operations had used their initial time of simply haunting him to gather up the initial stages of their candy hoard. By the time they had actually started stuffing their victim, one of his closets had already been piled high with leftovers that they knew he liked; they were at least kind in that regard. 

Phantom magic mixed with a supply chain of candy kept the growing hostage's mouth full, and ever able to consume more at the behest of his captors. His gut swelled out with every bag, bucket, and pillowcase of candy fed to him. Just like when the hands initially showed up, they subdued his will to fight back with relative swiftness, holding him in place and held each piece to his mouth frustration that was demonstrated gradually until he opened his jaw. 

Of course, despite providing a soothing grasp and touch to his gut, the mischief of the spectral hands that had taken up the charge of keeping his body soothed didn't exactly stop at just rubbing his belly!
