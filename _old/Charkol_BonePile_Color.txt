	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol had taken the opportunity in the late fall to go reside out of the confines of the city he lived in to let off a bit of excess energy that came with being a fire-type. Having skipped any semblance of a workout schedule for a month or so was the primary reason why he had gotten somewhat strung-out. Once beyond the limits of civilization and among the mountains, Charkol managed to make himself enjoy the first couple days of isolation by keeping himself busy with fire-breathing practice; the cycle of melting stone and subsequently recovering made time seem to tick along a bit faster. Just before the middle of his week away from home, he spotted smoke rising from what was a nearby highway. Due to the fiery nature of what he was doing and the limitations of clothing he wore, it was common practice for him to practice with his apparel stashed safely away. Not wanting to be of more immediate concern than he naturally was, Charkol donned his clothing and took off in the direction of the smoke. 

After traveling to investigate what was going on, Charkol had managed to assist with the aftermath of an overturned semi-truck that had been carrying various meats to some town he had never heard of. Despite the shape that the truck and various meats scattered about the road, the driver himself was relatively fine, sans a few cuts and bruises. Once the damage was assessed and Charkol being assured that there was little else to be done other than wait for help to arrive, the two chatted for a while. This ultimately lead to Charkol explaining why he seemed to come out of nowhere instead of coming from a car; his stress-relieving camping trip earned a chuckle from the truck-driver. 

Amused by Charkol's goals, the truck-driver offered up the meat in the trailer, explaining that it was going to spoil long before anyone could get proper storage out to the area. By the sense of not wanting to look a gift-horse in the mouth, Charkol accepted the offer, but planned to cash in on it later, when the drowzee's transport was going to arrive. When it was the best time to remove a few crates, Charkol hauled off a few boxes that held far more meat than he could ever consume into the nearby woods and brush so he could manage the logistics moving it all around when he needed to. 

Moments after Charkol bid his new acquaintance farewell, it became quickly apparent that there wasn't going to be any flying with the crates of meat. With little dismay, he resolved to simply shifting the crates farther into the forest one or two at a time until he managed to get all five a far enough distance from the road. Sharp claws and a little bit of brute strength revealed that each of the containers pretty much had uncarved hams, some plain, others seasoned, and a notable amount with the honey glaze he was used to seeing more of around the holidays! 

A few hours later, Charkol's belly churned as he intently stared at his last bone still not picked clean. The previously devoured pounds of cooked meat having delighted his taste-buds to the point of being able to easily ignore the tautness of his gut telling him to not finish his oversized meal. Even after so much, his mouth still watered and his nostrils actively drew in the scent of the sweet honeybaked coating on the meat itself. Tapping into his last reserve of patience to not dig in to the undercooked meat, he watched steam rise off of the flank and the sugary glaze drip off of it. If he were actually in a desperate situation, the rawness of the meat wouldn't have mattered to him; proper roasting over his tail-flame was only a concern of taste at this point!
