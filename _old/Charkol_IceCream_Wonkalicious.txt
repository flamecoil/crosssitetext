	===== FURAFFINITY =====


The summer often brings about a sale or few on ice cream, different flavors, quantities and mixed-in candies make for a vast selection of what to possibly pull from. Charkol, not wanting to just pick a few flavors, has somewhat been binging on a majority of the local ice-cream shop's stock whenever he could. The shop owners definitely appreciate his business, but are a little conflicted about whether or not they should mention just how many pounds the 'zard has put on!
