	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Kaida and Charkol live fairly different lives apart from one another, with the former being involved in more organized life amongst towns, pokémon guilds and mercenary groups while the latter has kept to clans and other less-structured organizations of pokémon. As a result, the two do relish time spent together quite often, sneaking away to various hidden places to share a moment with one another or simply retreating back to Charkol's cave to be lazy all day. The cave isn't just for lazy activities though!
