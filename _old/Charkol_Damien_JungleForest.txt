	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Although it's often by intent that Charkol and Damien cross paths, occasionally the two have business in a similar area, be it for hunting, social obligations or otherwise. Due to the distance between their respective homes, a chance meet-up rarely ever happens right after a planned visit. The timing in which they do cross paths usually means that there's a slim chance that the two find a quiet place to go to have a quick romp at minimum! This time, however, they had managed to find one another after concluding most of their obligations for the day and found time to relax before the sun set beyond the horizon.
