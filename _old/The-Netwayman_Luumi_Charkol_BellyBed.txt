	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Despite the amount of work that Charkol puts Luumi through while at work, the heavy charizard and bottom-heavy lucario have taken a liking to each other enough to hang out after the latter gets off from his long shifts. More often than not, leading straight from the restaurant to more private quarters leads to a night of relaxing fun, where Luumi can take his time with the big fella!
