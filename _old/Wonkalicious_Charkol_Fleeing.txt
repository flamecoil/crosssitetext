	===== FURAFFINITY =====


There's little that can be explained about the reasons why Charkol is haunted by what seem to be ghost-like hands; he of all people would like to know. Most of the time they're docile, and even non-existent in his life, only appearing once in a blue moon to vanish from his sight without any problem. Since they tend to refrain from affecting the locale of his home, they're mostly out of mind until the charizard returns home, and even then he can go weeks at a time not reminded of their presence. Despite their lack of frequent intrusion, they often do seem to have two odd habits they like to indulge in: Collecting or making food, and rummaging through Charkol's things for elements they seem to just not understand. Here, we see the beginning of their experiment with some of the concoctions Charkol keeps stashed away from one of his side-gigs after the fire-type returned home from work one day! 

Having baked a set of cakes as a presumptively tasty way to satisfy their curiosity, Charkol was haphazardly forced to consume a lineup of cakes that his ghostly cohabitants had prepared over the course of his day at work. With the weekend ahead, the floating hands knew quite well they could utilize the next couple of days to satiate their own curiosity towards the strange liquids Charkol stockpiled. They had wasted no time after Charkol locked the door behind him to start filling that gut of his with cake and serum, watching the later help his body metabolize the calories from the former with impressive speed! 

It only took an evening to go through a few dozen cakes, an orchestrated effort by the gang of floating hands to be able to stuff Charkol full of their baked goods one after another. Their concerted effort had put the fire-type into submission quite quickly as his gut swelled and sagged in front of him, the loud gurgling of his stomach churning food to turn it into fat quite audible and constant. Satisfied with their experiment, those ghostly hands shifted to making sure their unwilling host was at least taken care of as he processed physically and mentally what had just happened to him. Too exhausted to really care about fighting back, he let his belly get soothingly rubbed as he accepted his fate for the time being.
