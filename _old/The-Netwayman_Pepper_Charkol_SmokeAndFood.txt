	===== FURAFFINITY =====


When you've got a gal who knows how to chow down on their own time, it's not much of a surprise that they'll end up sharing some of their favorite spots to get a snack, meal, or feast from! Pepper isn't shy to expressing her like for Charkol's size, be it for what's between his legs or his hefty gut that seems to be looking ever forward to the next bite of food sent its way. Needless to say, a little bit of extra encouragement for observing his sometimes-ravenous behavior comes in the form of Pepper using her pheromones while she's feeding the big guy a meal, getting him more and more inclined to want to snack, eat, and gorge himself while he's around her! Much to their mutual benefit, Pepper ends up with a nice comfy bed and Charkol gets taken care of for the evening!
