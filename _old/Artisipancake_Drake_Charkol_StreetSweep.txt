	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Even though their combined weights more than able to make the ground shake beneath them, both Drake and Charkol with relative frequency take to town markets and go sampling most, if not all of each shop's inventory. When a certain food catches either of their attention, the two quickly make to clear out whatever stock the shopowner has. Sure, the two gluttons make a fair bit of a mess as they scarf down the food, but they always pay their fair share upon leaving; they're not thieves! Alas, being two big dragons might mean there's just a bit of coersion for the shops to sell all of their stock!
