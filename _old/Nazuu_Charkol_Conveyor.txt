	===== FURAFFINITY =====


When someone challenges you to display your ravenous appetite, you don't back down, right? Charkol here managed to completely destroy expectations of a lab technician that boasted about his line's food production to the point of making a somewhat joking bet that no one on the lab tour could last a minute in front of the consistent flow of heavily over-sized dishes for more than a minute of continuous eating. When the charizard queried if he could take on that challenge, other employees on the floor quickly set up an impromptu arrangement of conveyors, quite eager to collect data on their product. 

"Since we're not gonna take anything from you, your only punishment for losing is that we won't turn off the conveyor belt before one minute is up, and you have to just stand there!" the technician chuckled, laying out the 'challenge' just to have a bit of spectacle of just how much food there would be. Thirty minutes later, however, the group got a far different spectacle: Charkol's body had taken on all the calories the conveyor belt had to offer, meat, sweets, fruits, breads. All were devoured by the fire-type at a relatively consistent rate, with bones and bits being tossed aside for the lab workers to clean up promptly. Of course, with such rapid rate of growth came the futility of his clothing, something that a different technician promised would be replaced once Charkol was done with his feast!
