	===== FURAFFINITY =====


As Halloween approaches, Charkol pulls on his growing knowledge of humans to take advantage of the fact that some hoard delicious sweets at this time of year! Without remorse, he stalked and traced many potential hoarders of the sugary treats before settling on one recluse in particular, traveling out of town quite frequently with a set of supplies and candies to a cavern outside of a settlement. 

Once he had gotten used to her routine via means of careful observation, Charkol ransacked the cavern stash and took to succumbing to the demands of his sweet tooth and empty gut. After his claws got used ot the awkward process of ripping asunder the wrappers of human-made candies, the fire-type relished his field day, devouring what ultimately ended up being dozens of pounds of candies both large and small, liquid, solid, and some in between! 

Hours later, sugar rushed through the charizard's veins, putting him in quite the sugar high, but by contrast his gut was far too weighed down for him to want to do anything in particular other than hang around and continue rummaging through what he could get his claws on. Not far from the candy stash he had found at all were vials, vats and test-tubes of various sweet-smelling frostings, liquids and rock candies unlike the usual candies. Of course, natural curiosity guided him to slurping down a mixture of sweet substances without as much as a second thought! 

Needless to say, from the sugar to the unknown chemicals he had imbibed, Charkol was mildly incapacitated, and the subject to a concoction of mixed growths and aphrodisiacs! This lead to the charizard remaining in the cave much longer than intended, leaving him to only continue devouring more candies and potions. When the owner of all the alchemical reagents and candies had returned, she was greeted to a horny maroon charizard, with a swollen belly and a far bigger set of cock and balls than he should have had! 
Of course, few would be amused in such a situation.
