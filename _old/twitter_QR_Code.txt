	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Heyo folks! 

As the new year comes, so do more plans to travel! With two cons attended last year (BLFC, PAWcon), I do want to keep the number of cons I consider going to on the rise! It was quite fun meeting some familiar names and running into a bunch of random folk to make new friends out of! Online I'm not very social because I'm a bit too scatterbrained to keep up with who may or may not want to talk, but in person it's much easier for me to make a few connections, I think. 

First off, I wanted to kinda wave around a few points of contact, just in case anyone who's going to the same events I am possibly want to touch base and say hello! ( And grab a drink maybe? >:] ) 

• Telegram ( [url=https://t.me/flamecoil]flamecoil[/url] ) - This is probably my most open line of communication! Tap me here if you're just looking to get in a quick word or few. 

• Twitter ( [url=https://twitter.com/flamecoil]flamecoil[/url] ) - Another spot to drop a message, but beyond that it's just where I RT a lot of art that I see around! If you're not into w•hat my gallery holds then you might want to jump this one! 

• Twitter ( [url=https://twitter.com/Charkol92]Charkol92[/url] ) - I'll be using this twitter handle more for a bit of shouting into the void and potentially posting a few more socially-related things! I will be keeping the tweets locked down though, so most likely this will be for friends that I'll most likely see and people I've already met face-to-face; as such don't be disheartened if you don't get approved -- there's only ramblings over there! 

• Discord - I like to keep this chat client as uncluttered as possible, so rarely I'll give this one out for the sake of making contact with newcomers. This is mostly due to the fact that there's no Archive function like there is in Telegram, so I can't keep my chat list clean and my logs in tact at the same time. 


Next! Upcoming cons themselves! I'll be at least trying to hit up 3 this upcoming year: FC, GSFC, and BLFC, in that order! I'm not sure about if PAWcon will make the list again this year since it was definitely an ad hoc plan to drive on up! Alright, here are the plans themselves though for the 2020 cons: 

FutherConfusion (Arrival: Wed Jan 15th @ Night | Departure: Mon Jan 20 @ Night) 
I'll be in the San Jose area during the time of this con, mostly to be visiting a couple of my friends -- we'll most likely be dropping by the convention for a day or so and I might hang around the hotel prior to my flight out! 

Golden State Fur Con (Apr. 3-5) [My Birthday!] 
This'll be my first real-local con I'll be attending down in SoCal! I didn't catch wind of it last year, nor did I have much of a drive to go. For this con I'll be bringing a normie friend who's been interested in just how I chill about with other furs for quite some time! We're both registered so it's almost a definite go unless something real unfortuante happens. Maybe I'll drop a bit of cash and find someone to make a temporary 'sona for him! 

For GSFC I may need to nudge a couple folks to see if they want to split a room at the con, so if you're going feel free to reach out! 

Biggest Little Fur Con (Arrival: Thurs Apr 30 @ Afternoon | Departure: Mon May 4 @ Night) 
I'll be quite happy to return to the GSR for a few more nights of drinking, gambling and bowling! Last year I pretty much made last-second, impromptu plans to finally go to a convention after hearing so many good things about BLFC in particular. Even though I went up alone and roomed with people I had never met before, the whole visit was a success! I made some new friends, enjoyed some of the panels and quite a bit of bowling -- for this con in particular I'll have to make sure I reach out more and find people I at least want to say hello to and meet! 

For BLFC I do have a room currently. There is one comfortable spot left (splitting a bed with yours truly) -- gimme a nudge if you're looking for a spot to stay and we'll get to chattin'!
