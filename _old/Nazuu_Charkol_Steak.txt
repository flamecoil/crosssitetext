	===== FURAFFINITY =====


One of the best things about being a fire-type, is being able to cook, reheat and continually keep warm your food with minimal effort. Needing less effort to keep your plate full in turn means that it's quite easy to eat, and overeat, and just keep food on hand for possibly nibbling on! 

On lazy days with a surplus of food from the grocery store, it's not uncommon to find Charkol having turned a couple weeks' worth of steaks into an afternoon snack after cooking them all. His already heavy gut working to accommodate all of the excess meat usually encourages much lazier tendencies, such as lounging around all day! There are a few things that might be able to get the large 'zard up from his lazy perch though...
