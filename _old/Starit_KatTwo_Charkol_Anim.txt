	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol does believe that patience is a virtue -- putting up with others that get on his nerves has tended to save him trouble in the long-term. From time to time, however, there are a few that try to push his buttons for a prolonged period of time. Since Charkol is quite far from a saint, depending on how he's feeling at the time he snaps often determines how he exacts revenge on the antagonist. Being poked and prodded by a mewtwo for most of the day ended up interrupting the time the hugely-endowed charizard's time to relieve some stress. As a solution, Charkol knocked out two birds with one stone and forced the psychic type to help him with a bit of his daily routine!
