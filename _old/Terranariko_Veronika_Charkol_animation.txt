	===== FURAFFINITY =====


When you've got a good dragoness in your grasp that can boast being able to take your full weight behind each thrust of your hips, you put them to the test before they can realize just what they're in for! Veronica managed to approach Charkol with quite a bit of confidence, convincing him quite quickly to rise to the challenge of filling her up and stretching her out. By the time he was done with her, there was more than enough of a mess to have them both tuckered out, but most definitely willing to go for another round later!
