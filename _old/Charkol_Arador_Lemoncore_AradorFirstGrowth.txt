	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol's first notable roommate in college was Arador, a dragon who solidly outclassed the charizard in terms of overall size. The dragon's thick frame paired with his height was a constant reminder to him that the water-based dragon wasn't going to be all too fearful of him; a quality that Charkol truly appreciated in a roommate, considering that fire, claws, and sharp teeth rolled up into one package put many on edge. Ultimately, the two ended up becoming fast friends, able to avoid getting on each others' nerves most of the time while hashing out situations in which they did arise without having to deal with a prolonged conflict. 

One thing that the two didn't talk about for the longest time for their first year together was the fact that Arador was a bit more than just big between his legs, he was massive! Charkol had heard of hyper-endowments once or twice, and may have even looked it up on the web a few times for research purposes. Getting to room with Arador was a much different experience as he was pretty much confronted on the daily with what the dragon sported. Awkwardness over the months turned into curiosity, which then turned into a bit more, with Charkol taking enough of an interest to finally speak up on it. 

Inquiring on Arador's size was a little strange for Charkol, but after a roundabout way of first approaching the subject, he more bluntly reaffirmed his interest in just how the dragon ended up so massive. Although the dragon divulged the proper information, the conversation shifted to seeing whether or not Charkol wanted to at least see what it was like being so large! Of course, Arador was happy to oblige when he said yes, making sure he wasn't too overwhelmed by the sudden shift!
