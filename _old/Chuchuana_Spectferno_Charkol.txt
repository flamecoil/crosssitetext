	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Although Charkol is quite familiar with his own species, hybrids and the like of them are a rarity. Spectferno isn't a hybrid in the sense of reproduction, but rather spawned from a pact with death, leading to oddities and spectacles that the maroon-scaled male frequently fails to understand. His ghostly acquaintance, when she appears, is far from shy in his presence, often making sure that their brief time spent together is far from dull. Although not omnipotent, her abilities do lend themselves to observing more candid, possibly kinky moments Charkol engages in when he's not aware. He's not exactly comfortable with the thought of his own interests being discovered in such a way, but Charkol can't exactly complain when Spectferno is tending to all of his needs!
