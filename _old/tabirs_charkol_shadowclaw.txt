	===== FURAFFINITY =====


When words of compromise or warning fail to suffice, Charkol is no stranger to bringing out his claws. Living a territorial life makes each fight his potential last -- any mercy given too soon could end up meaning he some price of blood then or later. Fights induced by rage helped him along in picking up one of his more useful tools for ending a fight: Shadow Claw. A well aimed cut with this move can easily end a fight, or at least put Charkol at a significant advantage; the shadowy extension of his claws often cause critical damage alongside his physical ones. Although charizards are known for spitting flame, don't underestimate their close quarters abilities!
