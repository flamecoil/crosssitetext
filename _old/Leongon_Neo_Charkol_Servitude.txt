	===== FURAFFINITY =====


It's no hidden secret that when Charkol's used to being in a position of power, failure is quite intolerable. Despite his impatience though, he's quite willing to provide options in order for his subordinates to repent for their screw-ups. More often than not though, those choices involve him blowing off steam in one form or another. Here, we've got Neo on his knees making sure that Charkol's mechanical arm wont take a solid swing at him for the next few weeks at least!
