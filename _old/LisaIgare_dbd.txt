	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Lisa herself is a wingless earthen dragon who enjoys flipping the switch between working hard and playing hard. Very rarely does she not seem energetic about what she's doing, knocking down whatever tasks or goals she's set for herself down. It's not uncommon for her to twist, bend or break rules when there are situations in which normal conventions don't get her what she wants; resourceful and scrappy are what come to some of the minds of her social network. This enthusiasm bleeds on over into how she does like to make connections -- upfront and quite personal in order to hash out whether or not she's compatible with someone. 

When on the friendlier side of things, the degree at which Lisa tries to keep someone up with her pace is directly proportional to the degree at which she regards them as a close friend. Due to this process, some find themselves easily dragged along over a period of time, the gradual ramp up suddenly becoming exhausting when Lisa takes them out to party and drink for the umpteenth time in a month! By contrast, when friction exists between herself and another person, she usually likes to keep the other person in check; they will know that she doesn't like them for certain reasons. When usually she's the leader in most aspects, when getting into conflict, Lisa prefers to not be the one to cast the first stone, for obvious reasons. 

Although there's a notable amount of pudge on Lisa, it's not at all hard to spot that she's got more than enough power in her build to easily break a few bones if she's messed with. Although she's not one to frequent the gym, Lisa keeps her physical health on the mind, relying upon her own small assembly of home equipment to keep up with upping her strength and running around town to keep up her cardio. Ultimately, her outdoor presence is what gets her to cross paths with so many people, striking up a conversation or two while working out at the park or running into a fellow jogger; match that with the fact that she's 7'8" (2.34m), and it's no surprise to her that she's memorable! 

Lastly, there's not much hiding the fact that Lisa is pretty well hung between her legs. Knowing quite well that there are not many people who are relatively fine with such a large package upfront, she does make concessions to not always wear clothing that accentuates her endowment, but not at the cost of comfort. Beyond her general courtesies, if she catches you taking a long gander at her, expect a smirk and sly comment! If you don't have your wits about you might be looking down that needy length of hers!
