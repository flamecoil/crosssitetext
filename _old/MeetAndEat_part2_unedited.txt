Gulp...gulp...gulp... 

A few hours had passed since Sasha and Charkol had begun the task of of draining the large tank of strawberry pudding above them. It was filled only once at the beginning of their 'little' date and wasn't tampered with beyond that by the factory technitians. A few times along the way Charkol stopped to catch his breath and think he was full as his belly gurgled and churned with the sweet contents inside of it. Every pause he took came with a glance up at the meter above, the remaining contents seeming just too much for the two to finish. Regardless of their size, Sasha slowed her consumption and charmed Charkol back into eating some more. 

The shark's reasoning centered around the fact that she often enjoyed being of normal or larger sizes. Just as she knew ways to easily get to this size, the reverse was quite possible as well. In addition to it all, she pointed out that his body could take much more due to what else was in the pudding, a safety precaution for those who normally don't indulge in such a feat. 

"Plus, it looks like you're still enjoying it!" she added while pointing at him, his swollen, oozing length was still cradling the underside of his gut, fighting against its weight. "I think you'll be able to get to the end, we're more than half of the way there!" she teases while hefting herself forward and pressing her belly against his playfully. 

Charkol couldn't help but snicker a little bit at Sasha's continued cheeriness, it replacing the concern on his face with a smile. Shifting his own body weight to lean his gut against Sasha in return, he smooshed her own bright blue endowment between them as he settled back down so they could continue on their way to their goal. 

Having slowed his pace earlier, the charizard attempted to double-up on his rate of consumption for a while, wanting to match Sasha's size so he could at least say he split the tank evenly with her. His internal temperature managed to fight off the onset of any sort of chilled effect from the cool pudding: brain-freeze, chilled teeth, or a tight throat. Only stopping for a moment or two to catch his breath, Charkol's belly quickly gained on Sasha's as she watched with a teasing smile, occasionally swinging her tail to drum on it for encouragement. 

As they worked down to the last bits of pudding, the tanker above began to make a clicking noise -- a signal for the two that their inventory was almost drained dry. The pair looked up at the tank for a brief moment before taking their last couple of mouthfuls of the dessert to then sit in quiet content as their now-massive bodies processed what was left. 

"See? We made it -- and I think you manaaged to sneak in a bit more than I did!" Sasha teased after the fact, once more nudging Charkol with her own belly. The fire-type himself by this point had let the tube just fall from his mouth, panting a little bit as he really felt the magnitude of just how much weight was on his frame. He didn't exactly feel like it was impossible to move, just very different. 

Sasha snickered upon realizing Charkol's condition, "Oh yeah, you'll get used to it! I almost forgot it was your first try at this!"
