	===== FURAFFINITY =====



With every pancake sandwich that was stuffed into Charkol's mouth, there seemed to be just as many food items that were placed on the table to keep the stock high. Dom's hands were more than quick enough to be able to keep the charizard's mouth stuffed and keep more food coming at the same time. As his gut became more gravid, Charkol's chewing slowed, but never ceased as his personal buffet chef kept serving him; each mouthful of food accompanied by syrup-coated belly-rubs to ease that waistband-breaking growth.
