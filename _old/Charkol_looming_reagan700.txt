	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Although Charkol often doesn't like to put up with others that waste his time, he'll often give others a chance to explain why they might be interrupting him. His overall size sometimes encourages him to provide an additional allowance of time for those he's talking to so they can collect their thoughts. He's quite understanding that a looming look and a large belly might imply that someone smaller than him is on the menu for the day!
