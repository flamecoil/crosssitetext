	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Meat's a pretty solid staple of most reptiles' diets, and between Charkol and Krisa, neither are too picky about what sort of meat they get their hands on, so long as they're able to sate their hunger. Krisa's compelling nature, however, makes for frequent situations in which she put's Charkol's gut to the test, either by competition or just by constantly encouraging the charizard to have a mouthful of meat to chomp on, no matter how much his belly gurgles in protest. Natrually, with how Krisa works out with far more frequency than Charkol, the number of inches added to Charkol's waistline can be directly correlated with how much time he spends with his meat-loving friend!
