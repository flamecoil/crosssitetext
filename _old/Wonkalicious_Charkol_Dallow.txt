	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Although making body-altering drugs is a multi-billion dollar industry, one of the primary factors that keep the competitors few and far between is the process of finding trial participants once anthro testing phases have been reached. With the nature of the drugs themselves, brave souls that are willing to risk potentially semi-permanenent change to their body happen are notably scarce. As a result, over the course of time of part-taking n various experiments, some start getting familiar with one another. 


Dallow and Charkol weren't exactly sharing the same laboratory halls, they were working for competing corporations at the time they met. It was a public secret that any research effort at one facility quickly spawned funding and resources for an equivalent study at the other. Needless to say, on the rare occasion that the research subjects were able to go home before side-effects of the trials were completely reversed, many of the test subjects were able to spot one another out. At the time, both had residences in an apartment complex, which facilitated enough observation between them for one to make a comment on how ill-fitting their clothes were, a somewhat tell-tale sign of being subject to some sort of serum that at least had a side effect of extra weight. 

One thing lead to another, and both Dallow and Charkol took to sharing their own respective private stocks of company inventory. Turns out that Dallow also was quite fond of making baked goods, with a slight twist to 'em! Charkol was used to gorging himself on food during some experiments, but when he took on a challenge from the fox about just how many cookies he could eat with a fair bit of arrogance, he didn't expect to be pushed past his limit with such enthusiasm! Needless to say though, Charkol had become quite fond of his new experimental friend!
