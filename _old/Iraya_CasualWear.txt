	===== FURAFFINITY =====

Whenever allotted a day in, Iraya usually ends up trying to make it as seemless as possible to transition from bed to doing things around her home and back again. As such, she's taken to having her [url=http://www.furaffinity.net/view/24994320/]nightwear[/url] line up with what she likes to wear casually around the house and for possible brief trips out. One way or another, she has enough of a mind to know when she needs to dress up for the occasion!
