	===== FURAFFINITY =====

Studying both arcane arts, military tactics and putting them both to use ultimately has exposed Iraya to quite a few wonders and terrors of civilization. Be it on the road, in her home or in the brief respite between military campaigns, Iraya clears her head as best she can when she has the opportunity to. Although she'd like to say she was wise enough to practice mental housekeeping on her own, it in fact was a suggestion of someone who was outside of the arcane discipline. One of the higher-ranking officers she had been traveling with early in her career had picked up on her distress one day and offered his advice and mentoring during the times that they crossed paths. 

This piece was kindly done by @Jumanji_Oiyoiyoi as part of a freebie iron artist challenge back in March of 2016.
