	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Having taken a key off of Charkol's keyring a long time ago to grant herself access to his apartment whenever she felt like, Scylia was surprised to find the flame-tipped male missing from his home during his usual lazing hours. Usually, when the charizard was unresponsive, it was often due to the fact that he was a little engrossed in something, be it games, shows, or getting himself off. 

After a quick recollection of having referenced her friend Susan to Charkol for as a possible helper for [url=http://www.furaffinity.net/view/27912762/]restocking[/url] some of her supply, Scylia knew exactly where to go to find the other legendary and her flame-tipped friend. 

Since Susan was another legendary pokemon and maintained the local shrines that Scylia attended, there was little she could do about not adhering to dress code despite pretty much barging in on the rayquaza. Needless to say, when she arrived, Charkol wasn't expecting the lugia to start bickering with Susan over which serum to utilize in order to best increase production. Be it to the fire-type's benefit or not, the evening -- or however long he was captive -- seemed to go by much quicker when Scylia was mentally coaxing him to just follow along!
