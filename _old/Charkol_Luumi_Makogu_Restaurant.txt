	===== FURAFFINITY =====


There are few places out there that'll both serve the two sins of gluttony and lust, but when Charkol found a small place out of the way of his usual beaten path that allowed for both to be taken care of, he was curious at minimum. A private room, a plentiful selection of food and drink, along with a selection of potential partners for the evening made for one heck of a prospect! 

Upon arrival the heavy-set charizard was provided a room to sit and peruse the menu, it didn't take long for him to place a few orders. After his food order was submitted, Luumi, his waiter brought back a catalog of available partners for the evening. Charkol dismissed the lucario while he looked over each choice, mentioning that he'd wait for his food to arrive first. 

After all of his food and drink were laid out on his table, Charkol reached out and gave Luumi a tug on his waistcoat as he turnted to walk away, "What's it take for you to stick around for the rest of the night, hmm?"
