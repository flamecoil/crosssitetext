	===== FURAFFINITY =====


After getting used to getting around with so much extra weight on his person after being granted access to the royal stockpile of food that [url=http://www.furaffinity.net/view/25096977/]Drake[/url] has, Charkol has found himself occasionally finding it pleasurable to dine out in some of the more scenic areas of the territory. Strapping a large bag of wrapped foods to his body, Charkol has little issue making sure his gut is nice and stuffed wherever he decides to have an impromptu picnic! 

Of course, his gluttony does cause a little bit of concern for those who actually have to keep the castle stockpiled, but that's not Charkol's problem! He only has to worry about making it back home if he indulged himself just a bit too much!
