	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Like most charizard, Charkol indulges in life of laziness can be in most of the time since few dare to aggravate one of his kind one-on-one. Despite his calm demeanor most of the time, once he's wound up and ready to fight, the maroon male has no issue shutting down notions of mercy and compassion. Grudges form, hatred builds and Charkol will often surprise others who're not used to such a large figure moving so quickly! Once he has a focus for his aggression, he'll make sure that they're on their toes or dead on the ground!
