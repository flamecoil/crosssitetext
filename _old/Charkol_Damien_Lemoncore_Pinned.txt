	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol and Damien have been pretty solid friends for quite some time, the younger charizard having become far more interested in the maroon male after he found himself subject to permanent growth between the legs. Due to Damien's fascination with Charkol's experimentation with larger and larger sizes, it wasn't uncommon at all for Charkol to have his friend over for the night during a night of concoction consumption. Damien himself rarely had much of the growth-focused potions, but he was more than willing to be a subject of the neediness that resulted from them! 

Even the morning after, when Charkol was still too massive to be comfortable stepping outside, it was rare that he didn't force himself upon Damien as the other charizard was getting ready to leave to start his day. Knowing his time was limited, and that Damien did need to be able to walk after he was done with him, his preferred method of getting off was pretty much borrowing the smaller charizard by pinning him against the nearest wall! After a fair amount of grinding, instead of needing a half-day to recover, Damien often only needed a shower to recover from Charkol's assault, the only aftereffect at times being just a bit of that masculine scent not being able to be purged by just one or two rounds of lathering up!
