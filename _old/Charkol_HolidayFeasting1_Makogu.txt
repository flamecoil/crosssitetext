	===== FURAFFINITY =====


For Charkol, it's really hard seeing a lot of food go to waste. As such, for each plate that's made for holiday feasts, he is left with calculating out just how much food he needs to finish in one sitting to properly ration out the rest for scheduled leftovers. Stuffing his gut during the wintry season isn't something he's unabashed to do due the smaller amount of time he spends outside, be it by choice or by the weather's decree. Once he has sorted out his dishes by quantity and type, the fire-type gets to methodically eating what he needs to. Pies, ham, stuffing, yams, the occasional vegetable (often brought by a guest) -- anything that was left at his place after a get together needs to be thinned out to the point in which he could reasonably finish the lot within roughly a week and a half or two weeks. 

As the food finally approaches levels at which Charkol is fine with storing for later, his gut rests in his lap nice and taut with his day's worth of effort to pack it all down. Slight growling and churning noises can always be heard from the charizard's changed belly, but to him the excessive fullness is a part of the ritual! Since he is his own furnace for the duration of the winter, turning food into saved dollars on his heating bill, he often has very little concern for the extra weight in the long-term!
