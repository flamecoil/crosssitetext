	===== FURAFFINITY =====


From time to time, it's hard for Charkol to ignore someone who's as curvy as he is hung, and Marine is a mantine that far exceeds the baseline requirement to get a prolonged glance from the charizard! What took Charkol off-guard the most was just how friendly and forward the water-type was about having the same sort of appreciation for him as well! Before the thought could cross his mind, his new huge-breasted friend had Charkol by the hand and was dragging him off to have some fun!
