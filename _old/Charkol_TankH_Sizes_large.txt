	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Oftentimes Charkol sports a few different endowment sizes from something that fits his size and build to a magnitude that definitely is beyond the realms of just 'large'. 

This picture reflects the 'large' size for Charkol. If your eyes dip downwards while in conversation with him, you're bound to notice the hefty package. At this point, the charizard has resigned himself to making sure that he tries to minimize it's appearance under clothing so unnecessary attention isn't given to it. He's able to manage it most of the time, but he's gotten in the habit of forewarning anyone who decides to get a little too friendly of both the challenge and inevitable mess they're taking on.
