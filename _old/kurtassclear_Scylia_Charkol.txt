	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol is no stranger to traveling to odd places, however for obvious reasons his frequency to arctic locales is extremely low. Thankfully, the last time he found himself heading so far north that ice floated in the ocean, he happened to have a bit of a run-in with Scylia, a friend of Kaida's that often ended up in various places of the world as well. To fix the problem of the frigid air, the two decided to warm up together a little bit, despite the inefficiency of possibly laying out on the ice!
