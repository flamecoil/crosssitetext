	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Sometimes, getting a recipe right takes a few times to get just right. Not wanting to have a bunch of food to go to waste, Rogue invited Charkol over to put that sizable gut and appetite of his to work! In addition to being judge of each burger that came off of the grill, the fire-type help with keeping the grill lit with his own fire while the cooking was going on. 

After a long day of cooking, the two had quite a few burgers to get through, so they made themselves comfortable and went to work ensuring that nothing went to waste!
