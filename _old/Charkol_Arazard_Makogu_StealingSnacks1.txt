	===== FURAFFINITY =====


With hours to snack the day away on the weekends, both Ara and Charkol like to make sure that their stores of food are stocked up! Of course, with their combined appetites, occasionally the situation rises in which one 'zard or the other ends up a little short on the amount of food they acquired. This time around, early in the day Charkol found that the previous night's binge left him without anything to munch on when he rolled out of bed. With laziness dictating that leaving the house or placing an order for food were lower on the totem pole than just taking whatever food was available, spotting his blue-scaled counterpart, Charkol grinned and joined Ara on the couch: 

"Hey, you know you want to share some of that!"
