	===== FURAFFINITY =====

Flying while a little riled is an activity that most grown feral beings are familiar with, be it bad timing with the need to travel or just wanting to try to distract oneself with the current rut that they're in. Although it's rare to actually pass by so close to another airborne traveler, Charkol has no qualms with those who happen to get too close in the air and learn a bit too much about his current state. With such an opportunity though, it's not uncommon that the charizard makes an effort to court his newfound company in case they're in a similar state as he is!
