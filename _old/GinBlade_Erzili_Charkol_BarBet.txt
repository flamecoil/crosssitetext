	===== FURAFFINITY =====


A new job for Charkol often means a set of workplace oddities he has to get used to, not only with regards to the tasks and protocols he has to deal with, but also the social environment he's dropped into. Having a bulge that is nearly impossible to not look at, when taking on work that's more centered around customer service, Charkol has to figure out just how much he can joke around or call out attention to those who might take more than just a peek at him. 

As a bartender, there was a fair bit more leeway to cracking jokes and having honest conversations with customers as they down more drinks during the night. The rare few that build up enough confidence to poke and prod more into just how Charkol deals with his endowment occasionally find themselves talking to a charizard who's willing to let them see a bit more than just the bulge itself! Of course, this is all after-hours!
