	===== FURAFFINITY =====


For Charkol it's a little rough having a good friend like Kaida at times, not because it's a bad experience, but rather the things that the feisty flygon will do to him, from poking fun to wrestling and nipping at him constantly. Even though he hasn't ever seriously minded the roughhousing and and playfulness he was subjected to, waking up one day to his arms being handcuffed to the bed baffled him for a few moments, thinking it was simply just a prank! Plagued with a fair case of morning wood, the charizard struggled a little bit with his bindings, unable to see the culprit at first! 

However after a few minutes, Kaida slowly rose up from the foot of the bed and began to subsequently crawl towards the groggy charizard. Of course, with her lacking any clothing and a teasing look in her eye, the throbbing between his legs quickly shifted from being a case of waking up to one of heated arousal as Kaida snuggled up between his legs!
