	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Albeit very late, Be sure to go out and give all your friendly local charizards a hug, and share whatever marshmallows you might roast over their tail! 

Charizard Day lands on the anniversary of the North American release of Pokémon Red Version, September 28. This year marks the 20th anniversary for the game's release in the region, and I'm sure to no one's surprise charizard caught my attention from the get-go and has remained one of my favorite pokémon! 

Although I didn't get anything specifically for the day, it's always nice seeing the few pics that do pop up!
