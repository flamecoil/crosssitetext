	===== FURAFFINITY =====


It's been a while since I've actually gotten a new character, and with my first time having adopted a character, this raptor gal will be the first to not already have a character established behind her before getting drawn up! 

So far, I've decided to name her Leianna, and tag her with a relatively quiet personality. She's not really the shy type, but mostly engages in social interactions mostly when she's in a familiar setting or engaging in a subject that she's able to at least somewhat contribute to. Despite her avoidance of speaking up too often, there's rarely a time in which she just fully withdraws from other people; at times it might be a bit awkward for her, but she can make small talk!
