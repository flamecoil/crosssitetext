	===== FURAFFINITY =====


There's nothing more mentally distracting than being pent up after a week or so of a strict routine of eating, sleeping, working, and learning. Especially when sporting a sac that some people could have difficulty lifting, that critical threshold very much tests one's mental fortitude and habitual restraint. Those large enough know well enough the heavy churning sounds that can get a bit audible to those around them as one deviant thought can trigger a lasting, unignorable arousal that makes the present moment awkward for everyone. 

Charkol tried, he really did. It was a really good week, work and school completed and a long weekend finally approaching after having his head submerged in assignments of one sort or another. Keeping busy kept his mind off of the growing need weighing down his balls, but as a tradeoff he wasn't all too prepared for how he'd start reacting to finally being able to wind down with an activity that wasn't brushing his teeth or hopping in his bed to sleep. An assortment of drinks at a friend's new apartment, some card games, and a lot of camaraderie amongst the working folk that had earned the title of 'weekend warrior' since the new year had begun. 

As the night ticked on, and enough drinks were consumed to require some people -- including Charkol -- to crash at the host's house, a few guests began to tease and toy with each other, and the charizard wasn't spared of such treatment. Over games of cards, dominoes, and drinking, it didn't take long before the pants Charkol was wearing grew notably taut, especially to the folks that had been getting a little close and playfully teasing him about his size. Even while fairly intoxicated, the familiar throbbing halted whatever train of thought he had been on while playing the game and caused him to calmly excuse himself as if he needed to find a quiet place in the house to take a call. 

Of course, when one of the partygoers who had taken special notice of the growing situation between Charkol's legs, he found the charizard without a piece of clothing and barely placating the beast that had him fuzzy-headed! 

"Are you honestly that surprised?"
