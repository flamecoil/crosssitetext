	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Those who who know Rex understand just how mischievous the aerodactyl can be, be it from subtle jabs to all-out pranks and attacks with rotten fruit -- all of which are meant to try to get under one's skin. Thankfully for Charkol, the years of having known Rex have made him keep his wit sharp enough to either brush off or counter most efforts to get his temper flared up. Whether Rex fails or succeeds, there's always a solid chance that the large fire-type ends up a bit more aggressive than normal, often resulting in him pinning the aerodactyl down and giving him a good pounding! Despite the aggression, the 'dactyl doesn't mind all too much it seems since he always comes back with his arsenal of tricks to start the process all over again!
