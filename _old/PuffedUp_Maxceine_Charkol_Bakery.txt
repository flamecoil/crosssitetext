	===== FURAFFINITY =====


There's little that could be said that'd plausibly deny Charkol's sweet tooth and greed for free food, the hefty fire-type always licking his chops and swiping whatever he could when the situation permitted. When the head baker of one of his favorite stops happened to take advantage of those two traits, Charkol found himself at the end of a long-term testing run of Maxceine's new ideas and recipes for what she was going to sell each day. Needless to say, with every day being packed full of gut-filling baked goods and a new lightly-dressed zoroark friend, Charkol definitely was quite excited in more ways than one to chomp through the new recipes for the day!
