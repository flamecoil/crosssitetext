	===== FURAFFINITY =====

[url=Link]Previously...[/url] 

After putting on enough weight to effectively be contained inside their laboratory housing, both Charkol and Vigil made the best of their situation by requesting the delivery of a couple Switches. Although having some of the laboratory staff go pick up an online order for a switch costed a little bit out of their pockets, both of them were quite happy to have a method of burning time as the researchers figured out how to reverse their metabolic mishap. 

With how they were already comparing guts and groins by the time they had gotten so big, all the initial tests were done, there was no hesitation for them to hop on the only couch they had to stay comfy. Of course, they struggled to fit themselves on the one piece of furniture, it creaking and bending even though the lab had made it to withstand a significant amount of weight; possibly if only one of them had become so oversized! 

For the rest of the day, the lab technicians let them enjoy their games while prohibiting them from testing any more experimental consumables -- a result that neither of them had a problem with!
