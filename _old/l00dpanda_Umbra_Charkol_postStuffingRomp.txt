	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Weeks of gorging upon leftovers in feast like fashion proved to have quite the widening effect upon both Charkol's and Umbra's waistlines, guts, and overall size. Having tried most every food that their friends and neighbors could offer, the pair of fattened-up pokemon were far from shy about their weight at this point, not hesitating to throwing a full-course meal in the oven as they began to scarf down the one they had made only moments before. Each meal now had their first, seconds, and thirds -- possibly fourths if they were still craving something! Their gluttenous feeding habits fed off of one another, their willingness to clean off their plates together far more enticing over their bellies' long-since-muted cries to stop filling it. 

Even if their guts now were effectively bottomless pits where calories barely had a meaning, other needs were far from ignored. Having both been quite large in other departments prior to their quadrupling or quintupling of weight, Charkol and Umbra were more than used to figuring out how to working with their oversized bodies to satiate their hedonistic desires. Due to just how much effort and time it took to satisfy their gluttony or lust, it wasn't rare that the two ended up in a cycle between the two sins, just falling back on the other once the more demanding need was met! However, neither of them complained about their situation to say the least!
