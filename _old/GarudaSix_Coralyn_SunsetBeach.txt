	===== FURAFFINITY =====


With a belly or not, Coralyn has quite the affinity for putting on her swimsuit and enjoying a day at the beach! Like most of her kind, she's got a liking for the sun and can keep herself warm even after the big ball of fire drops beyond the horizon. As such, she rarely has any reason to change her attire as time progresses. From time to time, she'll even set up a little spot for herself -- and maybe a guest or two -- to sit down and join her for marshmallows on the sands while listening to the waves crashing. Just remember to bring your own beach towel if you're gonna join her!Â
