	===== FURAFFINITY =====


When Charkol found out that one of his long-time buddies had ended up having an affinity for crossdressing, he initially reached out in a slightly teasing manner just to have a bit of fun. After the initial shock of having found out, the charizard treated Gale -- the friend in quesiton -- to the same sort of banter as before. Ultimately though, the fire-type was curious just how well his fighting friend does dressed up as a hot date for the evening! A few additional teasing messages later ended up spurring on quite an interesting night!
