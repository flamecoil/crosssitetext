	===== FURAFFINITY =====


For Charkol, it's pretty much a fact that a couple of hot friends willing to indulge in a bit of heated action will always brighten his day, if not at least take him off edge. Charizards often catch wind of one another pretty easily through word of mouth, with their consistent being a fire-hazard, so off-handed comments or quesitons regarding concern of combustion nearby can quickly lead members of the species to finding one another. Sometimes, just quick introductions and networking exchanges happen, but thankfully for the big maroon male, he found himself making more than just friends with Chia and Ni!
