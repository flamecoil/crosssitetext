	===== FURAFFINITY =====

Despite having had a disciplined life at one point, Iraya is a little free-flowing when it comes to her sleep-cycle during the days she doesn't have anywhere in particular to be. Snoozing on and off until the crack of noon is not unheard of for her, and she quite enjoys it when she can do so. Of course, whatever time she does wake up, a solid chunk of time thereafter is dedicated to ensuring that she's nice and ready for the day, and definitely not just wanting to just stay in bed for longer.
