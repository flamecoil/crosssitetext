Although it's often a fire hazard, Charkol around the holidays often picks up the tradition of finding a Christmas tree to drag into his home to decorate. After being placed in the same laboratory campus apartment as Vigil, he quickly decided to drag along the protogen. Since Charkol's tail-flame worked quite well as a personal space heater in the cold air, staying out in the cold to grab a tree from the laboratory grounds was a fairly trivial task -- and that wasn't counting the fair bit of fat that insulated their bodies. During their early-morning trek out to the mini forest that the lab had set up, Vigil got to talking about other activities he wanted to do during the holidays while the weather was perfect for staying inside and food was on sale in plentiful amounts!
