	===== FURAFFINITY =====


To Charkol, there's nothing like a good charizard to have a bit of fun with -- the matching of size, durability and temperature makes life a lot easier when needing to have some fun. Darion, one of Damien's half-siblings, has been no stranger to Charkol's blunt advances in the past. Even though it's quite rare that they cross paths these days, the two tend to exchange information occasionally in order to keep up-to-date on any strange happenings in each others' territory. 

Even though both 'zards are pretty much willing to help each other out if the other needs a bit of extra muscle, rarely is that sort of help requested by Charkol!
