	===== FURAFFINITY =====


After the initial set of leftovers are put away, the stuffed 'zard doesn't exactly get to let his belly rest on the subsequent days. Although he knows he can make sure most, if not all of the food left at his place doesn't go to waste, meals still consist of plates filled with food, and possibly a second or third plate to keep on pace. On some nights Charkol does have the help of a few friends to help him clear out parts of certain dishes, but on the other hand some of those guests who do come over occasionally bring their own holiday leftovers that they or their families couldn't get rid of on their own. When it comes to the new dishes, thankfully his friends are wise enough not to try to leave anything not to his tastes on the list of his plates to clean. 

By the time winter really sets in, weight has piled on Charkol's body to a notable degree beyond just a little bit of a one-feast gut. Desserts often occupy his claws and meals blend together as he keeps his mouth full by grazing all day, heating up various leftovers be it by microwave or by oven to always have something available to fill up the empty spots on his plate. He doesn't hide this habit all too much, and for those who like enjoying the heat he can share while it snows outside, it's not unheard of for some visitors to bring a little extra for cuddling up against his larger form!
