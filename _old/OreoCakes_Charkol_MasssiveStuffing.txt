	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol had been labeled quickly both as a relativley docile and productive asset to the hunting party that had tracked him down after he had poached most of the food from a party stocked with a hefty amount of food. Granted, it wasn't all too hard for him to be found, his gut gurgling away at the massive meal made him an easy target for those that knew even a bit about how to track a pokémon. His strange tones and notable size quickly lead to a life of cozy captivity; food was at his beck and call, any ailments attended to, and no need was left unmet. As he packed on the pounds, his owners took to incorporating more experimental elements into the foods he liked, keeping him able to produce heavy samples of seed far in excess of what he was able to before. 

With an appetite that grew alongside his body's size, Charkol became a fair bit more demanding of food; constant attention was given to him as he oft engaged in long gorging meals. At some times though, that near-bottomless belly needed a break or simply his appetite slowed. Regardless of what he wanted to do though, sometimes it was time for a few extra supplements! Luckily for him, although he needed to finish quite a few pounds of food with what he was being given, he always was given his favorite pastries to be placated!
