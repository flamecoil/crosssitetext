When you've more than enough neighbors, friends and family that are willing to drop off near-untouched desserts, snacks, and drinks from their own holiday feasts, you end up with quite a few piles of food to work your way though. Of course, Charkol didn't mind, although throughout the post-holiday season his gut had begun to show that he was losing the war of attrition, always looking stuffed and bigger with each day. Although his belly wanted to wave the white flag of surrender at times, a few plates of dessert between meals was always welcome! Needless to say, the heavy 'zard had been keeping up on downing almost as much food as he was receiving!
