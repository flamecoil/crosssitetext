There's few things that get Charkol riled up more than a curvy-looking dragoness that knows what they want; however, with his overall size he knows that sometimes others bite off a bit more than they can chew! Veronica, pushed with unwavering confidence and eventually wore away at the big charizard's concerns. Of course, even once he did give the dragon what she wanted, he still kept an eye on her as he was enjoying himself!
