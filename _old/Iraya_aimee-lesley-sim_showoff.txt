	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Iraya's arcane markings are often hidden away by her ususal outfit of mages robes, so rarely does the dragon have a chance to show them off. Although they don't look like runes of any specific utility, they do have their own personal use to her -- that of which is a minor secret she doesn't like revealing without any serious reason to do so! Regardless of that fact, the dragoness does like letting others get a nice look from time to time!
