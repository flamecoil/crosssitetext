	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Be it to just let off a bit of steam, to intimidate passersby or fend off those who're encroaching upon his mountainous territory, Charkol has no problem using his wings and flame to resolve most of his problems. Having lived amongst the mountains for quite a few years now, the maroon-scaled charizard has little to fear. Word has gotten around that he's not a problem if left alone, possibly even friendly if you catch him at the right time. If you decide to draw his ire, don't be surprised if he puts the very familiar tools of fire and flight to use before you can blink.
