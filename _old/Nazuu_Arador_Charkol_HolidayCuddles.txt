The holidays are by far the time of year to catch up with friends, spend time inside, and cook up a storm of food to then relax by the fire. When living so far away from family, spending the whole stretch of the season away from work sometimes is a little untenable, so Charkol instead found himself able to enjoy his buddy Ara's company for many of his days off from work! From fighting off the cold weather with his own natural body heat to helping dispose of leftovers from some of the larger meals, these two found quite a few reasons to bounce back and forth between each others' homes in order to enjoy the holidays!
