	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Long hard hours of stitching, bloodying his hands and reading many books on how to properly become a mad scientist (including an evil laugh) were put into crafting a project worthy of calling a magnum opus this Hallows' Eve. Hauling a body heavier than himself wasn't exactly the most entertaining thing to do while the wind howls and the rain crashes down in Mother Nature's best efforts to fit a scene. Yet, when his readings and handiwork finally paid off with the first errant twitch of his monster's creation, all that was missing was a final crack of lightning to set the scene as Charkol put his evil laugh to the test with his wereshark monster standing besides him! 

Of course, why not add on a little something extra?  

Happy Halloween!
