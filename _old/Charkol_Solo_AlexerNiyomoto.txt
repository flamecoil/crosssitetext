	===== FURAFFINITY =====


When asked, Charkol has little qualms about showing off to someone who is curious, especially since most of the time clothing that tries to hide away his endowment in any way often is uncomfortable to some degree! Regardless though, if you do convince him to strip down, don't be surprised at the fact that if you stroke the 'zard's ego, you might end up getting to see a bit more than what you bargained for! 

This piece was done as a bit of fan art from falink / wlink -- they surprised me with this and a few other configurations of Charkol that fit one of his personal RP environments!
