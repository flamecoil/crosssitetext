	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol isn't one who commonly is seen with any sort of personal affections, eschewing any notion of the usefulness of equipment that others seem to deem important to wear as preventative measures. Having remained detached enough from guilds and humans, he mostly sees clothing or armor as unnecessary and a point for weakness since he has yet to really see much of an advantage demonstrated. It's rare to actually see him carry around anything that isn't explicitly useful, but one occasionally may catch him with a cloth bag containing a couple peculiar gems. Oftentimes if he's asked about said gems, he'll simply dismiss the question with a toothy grin and a chuckle unless he has deemed you enough of a friend to reveal his reasoning.
