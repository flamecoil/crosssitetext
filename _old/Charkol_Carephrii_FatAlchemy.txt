Although Charkol mostly sticks to what he knows, occasionally when the supplier for his frequented growth potions contacts him with the opportunity to self-test a new experimental batch, there's a fifty-fifty chance that he'll accept to be taken on the potential biological adventure that ensues. Needless to say, consuming the test liquid at that time was a mistake for the charizard.
