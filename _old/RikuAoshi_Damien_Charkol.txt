	===== FURAFFINITY =====


It's no secret that Charkol and Damien have a long history of committing incest, one often coaxing the other into helping them release some pent up seed. When compared side-by-side, most don't assume initially that Damien was Charkol's son with how few features are shared between the two chars -- and neither did they when they first met. 

Charkol had laid with Damien's mother after growing close to her over the course of a season or two, and due to her family situation, she urged the maroon male to depart and not return for the stability of her family. After events occurred years later that dismantled and scattered Damien's nuclear family, Charkol ended up meeting Damien as a rightfully moody and disgruntled charmeleon. Charkol didn't pry for what was keeping Damien unhappy, but with what few details he did manage to pick up, he knew that there had been enough rough times in recent history. Feeling a fair amount of sympathy for Damien, Charkol set up a deal with the charmeleon so that he'd have a place to stay in exchange for hunting and gathering food so that he didn't have to dedicate so much of his day to it when other territorial matters required his attention. 

Between Charkol unabashed behavior when aroused and Damien's curiosity, it didn't take long before the two were sharing lustful moments with relative frequency. On the contrary, it took the two quite a bit of time to finally cross the threshold of discussing the charmeleon's history and finally connecting the dots of Damien's past to Charkol's past through Damien's mother to finally realize that they were father and son. As you can tell, it didn't stop them from continuing on as they had previously!
