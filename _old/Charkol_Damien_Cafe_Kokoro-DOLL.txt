	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Damien and Charkol had been friends during their late high school and college years, with Charkol being a few years ahead in age. With how other flame-tipped fire-types were relatively rare in their area, they frequently spent time with one another to hash out any problems they had that might have been specific to their species. Although their personalities didn't line up very much, they ended up getting along quite well -- even to the point of helping each other figure out each other's preference when it came to sex. 

Although they were close friends, they didn't end up going into the same university, due to each having their own interests; however, both stayed relatively close to home, allowing them to either make trips back home at the same time or have a quick day-trip in order to visit one another. Due to this convenience, after Charkol had a long-term mishap with a science test gone slightly awry, Charkol and Damien convened about the status of the maroon male's endowment. 

Instead of finding the over-sized cock relatively concerning, it didn't take long before Damien proved to be the first brave soul willing to rile him up and let him have his way. Even after a rough night of making sure Charkol's heavy sac was nice and empty, Damien was no stranger to initiating the ever-growing charizard into grabbing him for a quick romp. 

Even years later, after Charkol had graduated and moved to a city that was much more accommodating to those living with hyper proportions, the two charizards rarely failed to keep in contact with one another! Upon Damien visiting Charkol for the first time in the new town, Charkol was taken off-guard by Damien having figured out that there were very relaxed ideals on what 'public indecency' was!
