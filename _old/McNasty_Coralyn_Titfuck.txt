	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Morning routines vary from person to person, and often are best crafted to ensure that one gets their day off to the right start. For Coralyn, the act of leaving bed most often the worst part of her day, so when she's got a friend who's a little more willing to shuffle about like a zombie while they wake up, she might as well take advantage. A bit of tit for that of what she needs to get her day going so long as she gets to spend a few more minutes chilling on the bed is a very small price to pay!
