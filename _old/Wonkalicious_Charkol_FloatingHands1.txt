	===== FURAFFINITY =====


After Halloween, there's more than enough candy tossed out or stockpiled in people's pantries, cupboards, and closets, all of it sitting in wait to be tossed out. Despite the holiday's passing, ethereal happenings rarely slow down; ghostly sightings, paranormal interferences and more oft plague the population in a more minor way than the nights leading up to the end of October. Mischievous spirits have quite a bit of fun at this time as costumes, decorations and that aforementioned candy are whisked away from their owners, most of whom do not notice. 

Charkol's luck after the holidays put him as the unfortunate victim upon which many of those spirits decided to act upon. By no rhyme or reason to their actions other than finding an unfortunate soul they found alone, the charizard was coerced to not fight against his new, floating guests. Although frightful at first, the fire-type quickly learned that he was relatively safe in his own home granted he didn't try to leave, or make contact with anyone else about what he saw. As the disembodied hands made themselves comfortable, they even proved to be somewhat benevolent as they took care of some odd household chores, much to his consternation. 

A few days of being forced to remain comfortably in his own abode passed, and suddenly Charkol found one of his unwelcome guests holding a bag of Halloween candy. It was late at night as he was winding down to go to bed, but the bucket was filled almost exclusively with the treats he preferred the most. Grabbing a handful of the wrapped chocolates, he munched away at a half-dozen or so of the fun-sized sweets before tossing the wrappers away and thanking the ghost for its interesting little delivery. 

Upon entering his bedroom, however, Charkol soon learned that eating a few pieces alone weren't going to keep his guests placated.
