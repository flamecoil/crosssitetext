	===== FURAFFINITY =====


As an early roommate of Charkol's for a brief stint of travelling abroad for schooling, he roomed with Aeshin for a semester or two, making an acquaintance with the dragon-shark hybrid only for a brief moment before moving into her abode, a lavishly decorated flat that very much impressed the young charizard. Even though they were fairly close to the same age Aeshin was far ahead of him in having a freelance tech gig and craft going on while he was still stumbling through what he possibly wanted to do in life. 


To scrap cash together, Charkol took more odd jobs around town, leadind him to apply to anything that might shell out some solid coin for minimal amounts of time. This ultimatley lead him to falling into what essentially was a lab-rat position, testing a fair few concoctions that were geared towards altering libido and sexual performance in anthropomorphic beings. Being the horny young adult he was, Charkol didn't bat an eye at signing away his body to this odd branch of science. 


Alas though, as he began to bring his work home, Aeshin at times got natrually curious and Charkol definitely had enough of an infatuation for his roommate at times to disregard the warnings to not allow others to imbibe the fluids he was carrying for long-term studies. Of course, they did always give him some excess in case of spillage or simple self-entertainment when he wasn't actively part of an experiment!
