	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol wasn't always sporting a lot of muscle. Before focusing on actually building his strength, flight helped him tone his legs and core naturally, so it gave him a fairly thin build to easily wield. Sure, he was able to haul a fair bit more weight than his appearance would let on, but he had no form of endurance after a first attempt. 

While in his first year of college, and an unsupervised adult for the first time, he took the opportunity to make some quick money by being a test-subject for a laboratory that was synthesizing serums for targeted growth. Over time some of the effects of the early tests turned out to be permanent, but it wasn't much to his dismay once he was offered access to a future supply and a paycheck.
