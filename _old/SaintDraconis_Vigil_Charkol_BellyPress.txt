	===== FURAFFINITY =====


As laboratory partners, Charkol and Vigil over time quickly got used to being quite comfortable with the oddities of delving into making their bodies endure heavy alterations often pertaining to growth. With both living on the lab campus where most of their needs were taken care of, the two ended up finding out that they were able to get their claws on some results of experiments that weren't directly related to their assignments, granted they were fine with additional studies were done. 

Having already gotten pretty gluttonous with their diet already with food being freely available, the prospect of trying out a few high density meals intrigued the duo. The tech was a continuous effort to try to make compact foods akin to rations more quickly consumable to provide the nutrients and calories they needed. At the time in which Charkol and Vigil discovered the option to take home food that was crafted with this in mind, they were surprised to find that most of the meals offered looked to be normal foods. Despite being warned that all the dinners and dessert items were deceptively packed with calories, they still stockpiled the product. 

Once home, Charkol wasted little time stuffing his face with what he could while getting comfortable for the evening. "Might as well see if it tastes as good as the regular thing, yeah?" was his reasoning for disregarding the actual intended usage to just eat small portions over the course of weeks. As Vigil playfully followed along in eating the dense product akin to a normal junk food binge, they both quickly found their guts weighing nice and heavy as the day dragged out! Pounds packed on and their bodies rounded out as each burger, pizza slice and tub of ice cream caught up with them in an exponential manner. Neither Charkol nor Vigil were worried about their newly found weight, and even compared their guts as they still had more food to get through!
