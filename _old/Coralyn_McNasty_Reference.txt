	===== FURAFFINITY =====

Coralyn is a charizard that sports a bit of weight on her, but rarely does it bother her at her current age. Although she's relatively docile and easy to engage with, she knows how to tune herself to deal with a variety of other people, becoming aggressive, teasing or calming based on whomever is the focus of her attention. With her ability to mingle with most people, the fire-type is rarely seen disheveled, or seeming too far out of her element -- she'll quite actively acknowledge and play along with scenarios she's not familiar with, but most of the time that's as far as one might see before she tries to gracefully disengage. 

She's yet to really find her place in the world for the time being, but she's a knack for going with the flow as life dishes her various occupations and relationships to pass the time with. Despite her willingness to do so though, don't press your luck too much with her 'cause once she sees a reason to drop the facade, you might find yourself on the receiving end of a blunt wake-up call to get your shit together while leaving her sight.
