When Drake first invited Charkol into his court, their time spent together was frequently occupied by overindulgent feasting, and lust-filled romps, the latter of which not enough to not keep the pounds from packing on both of their bodies. Gradually though, the royal charizard began to give his favored guest a choice for continually sticking around; he could prove his physical excellence by shaping up for possibly being a royal fighter, or part ways and enjoy the palace stockpiles only when a feast was to be thrown. 

Although Charkol had gotten used to his much heavier body due to their gluttonous habits, the varieties and quality of food he had access to at the click of his claws was far too much to pass up. After a few final binges of castle food, Charkol and Drake took to a relatively rough exercise regimen to bolster their strength and endurance. Neither charizard entirely lost their gut, but after many seasons of development the two charizards were easily able to put on grand feats of strength, while also still being formidable foes to a buffet! 

One thing that didn't change, however, was their habit of frequently having a romp with one another!
