	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Akiyo more or less has been one of Charkol's long-term friends, a little lucario that's never been shy about hanging about and enjoying a few moments in passing with the fire-type. Although it was initially a bit of a secret, Charkol found out that his buddy began to take a liking to his growing gut, in addition to the massive package he already had. As a result, meals they spent together grew much larger, helping pack on the pounds, while 'snacks' had grown to the point of being a full meal for most normal folk! Snack time, however, was also used to take care of another need too when Akiyo was around.
