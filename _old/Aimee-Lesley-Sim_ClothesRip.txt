	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Once Iraya became quite comfortable with a bit of transofrmation magic and managed to control just how much her body would grow, she natrually engaged in a few shenanigans with some old pieces of clothing she had no real need for. Barely making the excuse of 'needing to know' how her spells would affect her clothing, the dragoness took it upon herself to see just what it felt like to burst out of her clothing. Even though the resulting build-up and snap became a sensation she grew familiar with, she very much was wise enough to not mess with uncessary transformation magics when out and about!
