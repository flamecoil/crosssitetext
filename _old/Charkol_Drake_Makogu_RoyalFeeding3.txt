After eating away most of the day, Drake quite well understands the torpid behavior that Charkol had begun to demonstrate -- each additional bite he was encouraged to force down only making that gut of his weigh on him physically and mentally. Despite his sluggishness, Charkol did attempt to muster up a smile and nod every time he was given a question to answer or a little bit of affection from Drake, not wanting to be rude. Eventually though, the maroon male managed to throw in the towel as the sun set beyond the horizon, quickly wanting to lie down and let his gut handle the day's marathon of eating. 

Needless to say, even as the rest of his body had grown lazier, it was no secret that the mix of friendliness and affection between the two charizards ultimately put Charkol in quite the receptive mood to share a bed with his host. Unlike Charkol, Drake was quite full of energy and willing to expend it for the evening!
