	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Frequently, Charkol wakes up to the sensation of nibbling on one of his horns, with his mate Kaida laying, sitting or otherwise occupying some space on his back. Whether or not Kaida actually has a reason for doing it other than saying it's out of affection, Charkol finds the act slightly baffling. After having expressed his confusion, it only proved to fuel the flygon to make it a ritual to summon a roll of the eyes from the maroon male. Of course, Charkol doesn't mind it enough to do anything about it, but he would definitely like to know why at some point!
