	===== FURAFFINITY =====
Charkol_Donuts 

Although donuts make for a great sugary breakfast, there are few folks that would be surprised to learn that Charkol's quite fine with buying a dozen of them at anytime of the day and scarfing them down as a snack! If you stop by with a dozen to help him with his hunger (or help him with something else), he's always bound to appreciate it!
