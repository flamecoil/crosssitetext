	===== FURAFFINITY =====



As a tutor, Charkol often got times to himself in classrooms where he often was doing little more than twiddling his thumbs and looking up porn on his smartphone. When his hours allotted ended up being far after the hours of most classes, the charizard was far more forward with students that he had an interest in, figuring that if they were open to it, a bit of an extra reward for a good study session was in order! Of course, it would have been pretty easy to predict that Charkol would have gone after the cute charmeleon that needed a bit of help with his homework every so often, while also hanging around to chat for almost as long as his homework took!
