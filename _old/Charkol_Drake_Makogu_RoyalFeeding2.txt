	===== FURAFFINITY =====


[url=http://www.furaffinity.net/view/26653105/]Previously...[/url] 

It wasn't long at all before Drake had really stepped up on lavishing Charkol with food, drink and other pleasures of life in his company. At the demand of the crowned charizard, servents were frequently preparing platters of food for the two to split. The amount of food that was prepared, it wasn't a surprise that by midday the pounds of meat alone consumed by each charizard reached into the double digits even on a light day! By the time the sun was setting, Charkol rarely didn't have his belly's capacity challenged, feeling sluggish and barely able to down any more bites on his own volition. 

Drake, by contrast, was more than willing to help Charkol keep pace with him, not stopping the flow of food and very willing to give his maroon guest a bit of additional affection and encouragement to bite down on a few (dozen) helpings of food more. Of course, with both charizards spending quite a bit of time together and eating, it's quite natural that with hunger being a non-existant need, other desires become much more prominent!
