	===== FURAFFINITY =====


The jungle is a rare place for Charkol to travel, but he's not one to hesitate to the tune of extra coin in his pocket when a courier errand sends him down that way. Most of the time, the fire-type is able to take care of himself with ease -- between fire and flight there normally are very few threats that are willing to cross his path. On his way back home from his delivery, however, Charkol found himself at the mercy of an anaconda who wanted to get a little more acquainted with the newcomer to her territory. Due to a combination of the serpent's strength and tone of voice, she managed to both calm Charkol and introduce herself as Alessandra. 

Needless to say, the two managed to get along. Maybe Charkol will end up visiting the jungle a little more often?
