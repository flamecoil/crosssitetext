	===== FURAFFINITY =====


To Charkol, Dumisile is one heck of a handful to be acquainted with, but not in a bad way. The dino's eagerness to put up friendly aggression towards him makes any sort of outing with her a test on his nerves, whittling down until the large charizard snaps and retaliates in kind...and then some. Little jabs, nibbles, groping, tail-pulling and dirty-talking from Dumisile ensure that before the end of day Charkol is willing to pin his scaly friend up against the nearest wall and rut her until they're both tuckered out. Despite his muscled form and solid size advantage over her, Dumisile is definitely able to take what the large fire-type can dish out!
