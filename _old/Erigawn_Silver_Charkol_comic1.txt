	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol's no stranger to receiving looks out in public, even with the best clothes he can find, there's little to be done about hiding away his size. As such, usually when he catches someone gawking at him, he takes a bit of pleasure in teasing them with the offer of a touch or invite back to his place. 

Turns out Silver, was quite keen on both! Despite Charkol's offer mostly just being a bluff, the fox lit up with a fair bit of curiosity and excitement to get to see just what exactly Charkol was packing. Needless to say, this wasn't the first time that Silver had gawked at Charkol -- it was just the first time he had been caught by him!
