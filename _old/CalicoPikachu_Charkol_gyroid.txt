	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Being tossed onto a desserted island to build up your own community is a hefty task to say the least! Through recommendations of friends and a bit of nostalgia from one camping trip too long ago, Charkol found himself boarding a ticket on Nook Inc.'s Deserted Island Getaway. He may have not been all too happy to just have a tent alone with a couple of strangers on his small island, but the oddities and discovieries that happen when running around tending to the wilderness bring up quite a bit of intrigue! 

I've been enjoyin' my time with the game so far! Hopefully those of you who got a copy are havuing fun as well!
