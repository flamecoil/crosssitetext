Iraya is a relatively friendly dragon, one to be quite accepting of most, so long as they don't get on her nerves. Granted that she's showing her good side, a smile can almost always be seen on her face as she goes about her usual routine. Even with her cheery attitude showing most of the time, she's not afraid to resort to a display of distaste if need be -- she often tries to make her intentions clear with whatever she's going at and deterring others is no different. 

Standing at 6'10" (208 cm) without the horns atop her head, Iraya has a figure that puts her line of sight well over most average humanoids, but most likely average overall considering that many anthropomorphic forms are taller than humanoids among draconic species. Her tall frame is padded padded with a bit of fat, quite obviously not made of lean muscle, but by no means is she blatantly overweight. Even though she's a little bit on the heavier side, she can manage a fair amount of cardio activity before getting tired out due to having an affinity to travel. 

Outside of her casual clothing of comfortable pants and a shirt, tanktop or bra, Iraya frequently can be spotted wearing a set of mages robes. She's a practitioner of arcane and elemental arts, with the former being her primary focus in spells for analysis and manipulation. Her elemental skills aren't as great, but she has enough practice to defend herself with both fire and ice in order to ensure that she's not harassed while out on the road. In addition to her two primary focuses, she has a fairly wide arsenal of spells she likes to put to use infrequently, each being a simple spell or one she can quickly look up in a tome she frequently caries with her. 

Upon first glance it's easy to tell that Iraya looks like she's in her young adult years, but upon being questioned about her age, oftentimes she'll only smile and not provide a direct answer with a coy smile.
