	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Not having known many shapeshifters, Charkol wasn't one to really ever consider the fact that there was a possibility of having fun with them as different shapely forms of their default form. When Veldazik, a spacefaring friend of Charkol's, dropped by for a surprise visit during a moment of sexual frustration for the large male it didn't take much for the charizard to admit that he was struggling to keep himself composed. A quick suggestion and change of form by the kiirvalen made it pretty much near impossible for Charkol to not take his friend up on the offer of easing his pestering arousal!
