Although he doesn't prefer to live life permanently amongst more civilized orgnaizations of pokemon, be it guilds, clans and the like, Charkol has no qualms with the occasional stint of taking up residence with larger groups. Often working as a hired guard or transport, its rare that he doesn't make a friend or two along the way; usefull networking contacts for later work or lodging when traveling through the area. 

Some are fellow fighters, others are pokemon that catch Charkol's eye, and one trip north of the mountain range he called home, Charkol found himself in the company of a very boisterous feraligatr, Uhin. Normally, the charizard would have avoided such a noisy, and large, water type to ensure that his stay was relatively uneventful besides the job he had been summoned for. What changed Charkol's mind was the involuntary inclusion of his presence in a successful mission celebration that had been going on at the tavern while he was there. Uhin presented a drink to the newcomer, and insisted that he wasn't going to leave until the drink was gone. Not being one to turn down a bit of hospitality, Charkol finished the drink and before he could set his stein down, another was there, with Uhin grinning and already having taken a seat next to him. Unsurprisingly, the most frequently uttered words between the two as they chatted away were: "Bring me another!" 

To few patrons' surprise, when the celebration had gotten well underway both Charkol and Uhin had taken a fair liking to one another!
