	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Having little knowledge about salazzle himself, Charkol had pretty much only the base knowledge that Pepper was a pokémon as well but no real clue about just what the species entailed. With the nature of trying to fully memorize every single addition to the classification above his species, the charizard had long since given up on prodding a pokémon species he hadn't seen with mundane questions beyond simple typing. 

Pepper proved herself to be quite unafraid of Charkol's size, both with regards to height and endowment, which was appreciated due to the fact that the less attention that was diverted below his waist, the easier it was to meet new people. What he didn't expect though, was what happened after her attention was fully focused on his unavoidable package...He ended up learning quite a bit about salazzle that day, and for a few days after that!
