	===== FURAFFINITY =====

Being a fire-type, one skill that Charkol developed throughout his adolescence was controlling his body's temperature. From anger to excitement, he used to easily spike his body heat; on a bad day his body could singe, burn or melt materials not exactly made to withstand contact with anything far above the average body temperature. Although Charkol is now practiced enough to not have to worry about burning a partner he decides to have sex with, finding another charizard to rut always provides a bit of an extra kick to the moment! 

Once Maiya and Charkol confirmed their interest in one another, the two quickly took to letting their flame-related inhibitions go -- smoke, embers, and the occasional plume of flame accompanied the rise of their arousals. Without so much as a flinch from Maiya at Charkol's size, the pair enjoyed quite the rough evening with multiple rounds around Charkol's home. By the end of it all, both charizards were worn out and sated!
