	===== FURAFFINITY =====


After a previous run-in with a teasing, antagonistic creature that ended up being subject to Charkol's base needs, the charizard quickly took to making sure his trip I'm an unfamiliar land didn't mean when that his heavy, often pent-up balls didn’t go unsated for long. The day after filling up Yevi, Charkol came across a much more curious creature that seemed of similar build to the prior antagonist. Much to his delight though, it didn't take a pursuit and lots of scorched earth in order to get a hold of his new plaything. Considering Charkol's sheer size, it was a slight bit of effort to get himself going with the carbuncle, but with how those purple paws took to rubbing over his scales, the fire-type took his sweet time having his way. 

By the end of it all, there was more than enough mess to leave Vin stuffed for a long while! Of course, considering how well Vin seemed to take the treatment, Charkol made a mental note to drop by the same area in hopes of finding the eager-to-please critter at a later date!
