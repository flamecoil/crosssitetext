	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol, albeit being used to being the tallest one in the room more often than not, isn't completely unfamiliar with having someone being able to stand a head or so over him. What he didn't expect, however, was the first time he had someone who so demandingly gain his acquaintance! Sinu, a dragon hybrid that very much appears to be of some astrological heritage, managed to both impress and entice Charkol into her social circle. Since Charkol was somewhat able to hold a frame to half of her normal height, he proved to be able to handle both her company and most tasks that she asked of him. 

Between her bluntness, teasing, and alluring lifestyle of casualness and luxury, Charkol often found himself in the position of being rewarded for helping the large dragon hybrid out. Sometimes though, their intriguing friendship has moments like this in which the charizard is more than confused, but he has no reason to deny her! Being one to forget small details, a slight slip-up in a fast-food order has Charkol as an impromptu fill-in for said detail! 

"I told you I wanted mayo! make yourself useful and give me what I want."
