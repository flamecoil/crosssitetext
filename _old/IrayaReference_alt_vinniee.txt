When pushed to the brink of exhaustion by extensive use of magic, Iraya occasdionally resorts to taking a selfish route to ensuring that her mana is swiftly restored to a comfortable quanity. Upon casing a short ritual, her scales shift notably, the cool blues of her scales shifting to whites and vice versa on some parts of her body. Her physical structure doesn't change, but granted one were looking forh er based on scolor scheme alone, it might be hard to recognize her. 

The ritual itself doesn't take much, the materials needed for it are often cheap and can be focused around an empty mana container -- often a crystal for simplicity's sake. Usually the ritual itself is completed alone; once it's done, her entire being becomes a centerpoint for most life around her, slowly draining magical energy from entities nearby. Those who are particularly weak can fall subject to having their life-force drained by accident; in most cases, augmentation to the effect would be needed to drain most entities. 

While in this state, Iraya's non-vampiric abilities are hampered a fair amount, but she's not without them. Ending the ritual itself does require a substantial amount of mana, so once it's initiated, she often remains in this form for some time. Although her personality doesn't directly change based on this form, being quite mana-starved has been known to put Iraya into a bit of a sour mood; it's been reported that she can get a little antagonistic when trying to regain her mana reserves.
