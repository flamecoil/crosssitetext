	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Friends who talk a fair bit about their sexual prowess often get Charkol's attention pretty easily, the big charizard quite unabashedly sizing up anyone who even offhandedly mentions that they might be able to take the bit of roughness that he can dish out. Whether it be from his raw size, or the power that his legs and put out during a heated romp, there's quite a few criterion in which Charkol could possibly rule out having a bit of fun with some of his smaller-looking friends. Even as a full-fledged adult, Volt wasn't exactly a lucario that had height on his side, being a meager four-foot pokémon while Charkol nearly doubled his height. 

Charkol though isn't one to turn down someone looking to challenge themselves, especially when his balls are feeling quite heavy! 

"Well, look at that. You made it to the end, although somethin' tells me you're gonna need me to carry you."
