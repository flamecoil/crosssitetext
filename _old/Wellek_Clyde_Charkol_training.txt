	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Clyde Andros' luck for being the son of a Kanto Champion allowed for him to have quite a few benefits during his upbringing -- exposure to the high-energy of the leagues the various regions, seeing powerful, rare pokémon up close and having a wealth of knowledge about pokémon pretty much in the next room when his father was home. 

Regardless of his exposure, the shark was a very late bloomer when it came to interest in his own journey in the world of pokémon, but when he finally was willing to take his own shot at seeing it all for himself, his father handed over Charkol as a parting gift. Although Charkol was one of his father's pokémon, he was not raised domestically, so he retained some feral behaviors. With his father gone, Clyde wasn't all too clear on the specifics of how to take care of a pokémon of Charkol's stature despite being quite familiar with having the large male occasionally around the house in Goldenrod City, Johto. 

Delaying the decision to venture outside of the city limits, Clyde kept Charkol well-fed in his home for some time. Over time, Charkol's behavior became unruly due to being long-overdue for a trip to a daycare center for a romp to level out his arousal. Sight of the horny male eventually spawned uncertainty about what to do, and letting that go on for too long quickly educated the feminine shark on what specifically happens when Charkol's need is left not sated! Needless to say, Clyde now knows how to handle Charkol in such times and has yet to stop by a daycare center!
