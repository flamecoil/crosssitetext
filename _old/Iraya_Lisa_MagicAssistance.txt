	===== FURAFFINITY =====


During Iraya's military service to her clan as a logistics and intelligence analyst, it was rare for her to actively take part in operations with those who were part of the ground forces that engaged in full-on combat. On rare occasion though, when information brought to her her specific unit was confirmed and needed to be delivered back, Iraya would have the privilege of jumping into the far larger social environment of some outpost or encampment. 

Although the cultures between the main military force and more information-focused divisions were quite different, Iraya never felt too out of place when running between camps to deliver the relevant information to the necessary personnel. With what little time Iraya had to socialize amongst the larger crowd, she often made her way to show up and participate in games, drinking and basic combat practice. Due to those activities, she made fast friends with certain groups of soldiers. 

Lisa Igare was one of the few that managed to consistently catch Iraya on her rare visits to her encampment, since her battalion often relied upon making moves with the information provided by the mage's unit. Often, friendly favors were exchanged between the two, money, spoils and trinkets found during their travels and the occasional venting session, be it emotional or physical!
