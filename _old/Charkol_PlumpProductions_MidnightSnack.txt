	===== FURAFFINITY =====


When waking up in the middle of the night, two disturbances are quite common for the heavy-set fire-type: arousal and hunger! With his gut growling to be filled and his balls gurgling to be emptied, usually the former is taken care of by a quick trip to the kitchen, stacking a plate or few high with leftovers and desserts to enjoy back in his room. Of course, there's rarely anything keeping him from taking care of his other need while enjoying his late night meal!
