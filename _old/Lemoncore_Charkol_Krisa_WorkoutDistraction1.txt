	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Meeting Krisa was quite the surprise for Charkol, the two had met pretty much in passing as two strangers with little to connect each other beyond their common location. A quick hello turned into small talk, which then escalated into Krisa giving the chubby charizard a challenge and pat on the gut! Although the fire-type hadn't done anything resembling a proper workout routine beyond keeping his wings and arms strong, the confidence in which Krisa egged him into testing himself out pretty much ensured his participation. 

The following weekend, Charkol found himself dressed in new athletic clothing -- both belly and bulge had gotten far too large for his older gym shorts, while the shirts just barely fit. Needless to say, with Krisa delighted at her challenge being met, the two took off on a nice run, with the t-rex wagering a bit of a reward of he could keep up! 

Sometime later, after Charkol was far out of breath, sore and quite ready to limp on his way back home, Krisa made good on her promise by quickly making it impossible for Charkol to not remain in his running shorts!
