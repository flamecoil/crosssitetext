	===== FURAFFINITY =====

It's not uncommon that Iraya has long days planned out, from the time she decides to actually roll out of bed to the hours after sundown, the dragoness keeps her days quite busy in order to maximize the chance of a lazy day off in the future. When one of those days comes to an end, there's often little time for her to do anything but strip down, flop on her bed and decompress. At times, she ends up too lazy to fully undress before laying down and takes her sweet time getting rid of any articles of clothing or other accessories off of her person before going to sleep.
