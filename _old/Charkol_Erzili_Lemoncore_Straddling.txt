	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Greninja aren't too uncommon for Charkol to run into, the occasional greeting in passing or conversational partner while waiting for a common arrival, be it food, transport, or just for time to pass. Erzili though drew Charkol's attention while at the gym -- her unusual outfit and movement to compared to with how he perceived most modern greninja was enough for him to act upon his curiosity. 

Not being too far off from twice her height, Charkol was careful to approach from an easily visible angle, not wanting to startle Erzili while she was focused on her exercises. Having been used to getting uncomfortable stares from new people he met, it was his turn to be a little surprised as he was given a very calm welcome with a focused stare. 

The two quickly became acquainted, coordinating their times at the gym (rather, Charkol finally started showing up on a regular basis), to train and spar with one another. Each of them satisfied the curiosity of the other, answering questions about background, fighting style and obvious traits that stood out. As a friendship grew, Charkol found himself being used occasionally as a mode of transport for Erzili; he quickly discovered that even with her curvy form she was quite light while riding around on his back or shoulders. 

It didn't take long after Erzili began to ride around on Charkol did she casually begin to use said mode of transport to leave the gym with him. Even though they spent time outside of the gym as friends, the greninja kept up her calm and collected demeanor, even when teased or prodded at by Charkol for her mannerisms. Over time, he managed to learn how to read her personality and coax her into occasionally cracking a smile or allowing for a hug. Persistence does pay off though, and when the charizard needed a partner for some other vigorous activity, Erzili ended up willing to help, much to his surprise!
