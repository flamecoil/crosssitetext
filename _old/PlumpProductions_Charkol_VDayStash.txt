	===== FURAFFINITY =====


The days right after Valentine's Day are quite bountiful for anyone who likes a good deal on candy, this is no secret. In combination with his friends tossing him the spare candies they couldn't stomach to finish, Charkol made his rounds quite successfully to get a stash of candy that normally would last some time. Alas, in the weeks immediately after the holiday, Charkol wasn't exactly disciplined in not cracking open his stockpile every few minutes or so. Now, with his last barrel of candy already being worked through, the heavy 'zard figured he might as well sit down and enjoy it instead of worrying about just how to pace himself!
