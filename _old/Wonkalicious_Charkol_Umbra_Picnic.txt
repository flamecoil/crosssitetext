	===== FURAFFINITY =====


When you've got far too much food and a friend that's willing to help you finish it off, you definitely shouldn't let that food go to waste! Charkol is a firm believer in leftovers, be it his own or taking home his friends' leftovers when he's invited over for a meal. On rare occasions, there are times in which the charizard acumulates more than even he knows his gut can handle in one sitting, but is more than willing to split it up over multiple tries. One of his more sizable friends, Umbra, found his dedication quite amusing and nonchalantly suggested that she wouldn't mind risking a few extra pounds to just see how much food they could stuff away together. Although he was quite surprised at the umbreon-hybrid's openness to the idea, he didn't deny her in the least bit, following thorugh with setting up a date and time for them to settle in and gorge for the day! 

Main courses, side dishes, appetizers, and desserts littered the floor of Charkol's apartment when Umbra arrived. With how experienced the charizard was a gorging himself on excessive amounts of food, he informed his newfound foodie-friend that there wasn't going to be much in the way for need of every single article of clothing -- shirts and pants were gonna get stains and with as much pudge they were going to put on, only underwear was going to be comparatively easy to wash. Taking Charkol's word for it, Umbra ditched her own outerwear to match the charizard clad only in what could be considered a sling that struggled to contain his package. After that, they both laid into the various hot dishes that Charkol had rotated in and out of the oven to keep warm, and munched for many hours, only pausing occasionally to let their guts digest some along the way. With their persistence -- and the help of a little bit of extra scientific serum, as Charkol is known to have -- their bloated bellies and plump rumps managed to see their way through to the desserts and keep on going!
