	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol's shark-friend Clyde had been one of the few acquaintances that the large charizard could count on to be quite fine with hosting up and over his shoulder and carrying him to bed for a solid romp every so often. Occasionally, Clyde took it upon himself to occupy Charkol's bed while he was away to easily acquire some additional warmth in the middle of the night when the larger male retired. 

Much to Charkol's surprise, the form just barely under his covers one night was far larger than what he had come to expect, considering Clyde's normal size often barely made a difference amongst the many comforters that were present on his bed. Leaping out in an effort to surprise him -- that of which worked -- Clyde made it pretty clear that his new monstrous form wasn't threatening, sans the size overall and the much heavier endowment between his legs! 

Clyde's new wereform never was explained to him, but Charkol quickly surmised that the shark had gotten into his stash of concoctions that he received as a result of being the subject of a slightly-failed experiment! Of course, these fluids were intended for enhancing performance with regards to sexual abilities, but they caused growth when consumed in excess. The excited and fairly pent up shark challenged the fire-type to downing as much of the experimental fluids as he could before needing to take a break and let off some stress, that of which Charkol agreed to in good humor. 

After the two had hit their consumption limit, it was quite apparent that Clyde's new body was far superior with regards to processing and handling the effects of the fluids without needing to release!
