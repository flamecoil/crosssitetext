	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charizards aren't all too common one way or another, much less ones that sport a bit of extra heft akin to Charkol, and even less so ones of the female variety. As such, when finding himself in the company of a fellow friendly charizard that was more than interested in him he got a bit excited, which lead to a bit of showing off. Considering he was big all around, it was a bit hard to figure out what specifically she was focusing on, but Charkol enjoyed the attention nonetheless!
