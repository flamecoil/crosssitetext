	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Psychic pokémon seem to have a knack for figuring out just what you need, when you need it. Whether or not they might be using their psychic powers to read minds or mischievously cause said needs, it's all quite fine so long as they help out in the end -- which it seems like Pat was quite happy to do for Charkol! 

This pic was purchased as a quick gift for LaPatte indirectly convincing me to head out to my first furry convention, where I had a great time meeting a bunch of folk!
