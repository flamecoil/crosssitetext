	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Almost all the time, Charkol is used to being on the taller side of all of his partners, while also being more eager for a romp simply due to his hefty package always needing a bit of relief! Shazla, an aquatic dragon that clearly towers over Charkol, has no problem teasing and toying with the heavily-endowed charizard and even initiating a good bit of fun when he can't take it any more!
