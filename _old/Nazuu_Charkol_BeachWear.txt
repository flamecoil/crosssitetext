	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Albeit a fire-type, Charkol doesn't shy away from the beach during the hotter times of the year! Although he doesn't have the ideal beach-body, there's little that stops him from getting his toes in the sand clad in some swimwear, a simple shirt and a pair of sunglasses. More often than not, the fire-type picks up a drink or few while meandering amongst the crowds at the beach-front stores. As he walks around, it's quite obvious that there's not much left to the imagination, acknowledged by him with a sly, toothy grin when he catches someone staring!
