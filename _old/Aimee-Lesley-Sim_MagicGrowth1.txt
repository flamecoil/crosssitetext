	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Although most, if not all of Iraya's magics have been picked up as a utility to help herself out in the field or day-to-day life, she still occasionally expresses curiosity in certain classes of magic she's not developed her skills in. More often than not, the dragoness has access to a few friends, libraries or tomes in her own collection that at least offhandedly teach the basics of what she wants to dabble in for the afternoon. Having never been interested in the toll that transformative spells have on the body, Iraya's curiosity is what drove her to give a quick attempt at experimenting a bit! 

After doing some studying, Iraya gathers her focus, energy and bodily fortitude to see just how well she can control this new spell!
