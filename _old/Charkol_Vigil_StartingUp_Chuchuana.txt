	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Although most of Charkol's interaction with the company that supplies him the various potions for growth is through shipments, lab appointments and various visits by technicians when the occasional experiment goes wrong, once in a blue moon Charkol does go into the lab to see who might be some new faces around the facility. Upon picking up a new shipment of supplies, Charkol quickly made friends with a little protogen that was pretty much brand-new to the facility. After taking a liking to the cute little living machine, Charkol nudged a few staff members for clearance to have a bit of fun with the new subject, and in no time he had Vigil back at his dormitory on the lab campus. 

Working through a few different types of serums together eventually lead to a point in which Charkol was on the receiving end of some very intent-driven affection from his guest -- two large, glowing spires pretty much threatened to tap him on the snout as Vigil slowly began to get a taste of Charkol's one. Letting the protogen take his time, Charkol took quite a bit of enjoyment from just how receptive Vigil was to consuming the concoctions, expressed by a warm touch to Vigil's now much more prominent backside. Needless to say, Charkol had much more in store for Vigil!
