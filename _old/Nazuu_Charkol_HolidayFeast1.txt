	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol isn't one to shy away from storing away leftovers. Whenever he cooks he intends to make enough for the week, extra-large orders of food get boxed up and tossed in the fridge, even at parties with good friends the large fire-type eyes solid choices that might be good enough for him to carry on home! This, in combination with the holidays sometimes produces a bit of a heavy stockpile that can't easily be worked through in a reasonable amount of time. 

Despite the burden of overstocking, he still has a habit of encouraging his friends to drop off any mostly in-tact dishes that he can reheat for later. Every once in a while though, in order to avoid wasting his stockpile, the big guy sits himself down and gets to work clearing out what his gut will allow him to! Of course, this doesn't stop him from welcoming new dishes to keep!
