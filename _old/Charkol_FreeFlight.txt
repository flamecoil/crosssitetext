	===== FURAFFINITY =====

Despite the hassle of indulging in such a pleasure in modern society, Charkol does enjoy flyin' nude from time to time! Instead of simply strolling out his front door in the nude, he'll often travel out to a relativel remote and senic area that's a relativley short drive from his hometown. Once relatively sure that he's far enough away from most well-traveled roads, the charizard takes flight, with most, if not all of his clothing left behind. 

Of course, just becuase he's far off from town, it doesn't mean that there are coincidental observers at time to see Charkol fly on by! It's happened often enough for the fire-type to abandon the chagrin behind the encounter itself; often he just greets those he runs into with a playful smile or gesture as he continues on his way!
