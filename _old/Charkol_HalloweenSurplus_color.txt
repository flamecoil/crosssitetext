	===== FURAFFINITY =====


It's been a week or so since Halloween has wrapped up, and with the lack of demand for all the candy left in the supermarkets comes the sales to clear them out! Since he had already worked through his own personal stash of leftovers after the visits from trick-or-treaters, Charkol didn't hesitate to stockpile a few dozen pounds of candy to hold himself over until the holiday baked goods were occupying the shelves! After a gorging upon a lot more candy than usual, the fire-type found his clothes already struggling with containing his gut, but to him it didn't matter all too much -- a few clicks and more fitting clothing would be on its way!
