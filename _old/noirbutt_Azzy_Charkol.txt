	===== FURAFFINITY =====


"You don't need that many for yourself, now do you?" Charkol playfully chided, wearing a smug smile as he looked down upon Azzy, who lived on the same floor of his apartment residence. Whether or not it was the large bulge, gut or just his playful introduction to his neighbor, the two ended up getting pretty comfortable pretty quick! 

To knock out two birds with one stone, Charkol got to know Azzy over the few shared boxes of donuts that were brought along while testing a few metabolic serums that he had received from the lab he worked at. The serum itself aided the breakdown and storage of the donuts' caloric value, quickly sizing the charizard up around the waist and belly such that Azzy could rest upon it even with so many donuts to go!
