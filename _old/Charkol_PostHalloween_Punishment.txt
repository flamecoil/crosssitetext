	===== FURAFFINITY =====


[url=http://www.furaffinity.net/view/25170382/]Previously...[/url] 

After having worked his way into a witch's stash of candy for Halloween, Charkol apparently hadn't been seen for quite some time afterwards. The issue with stealing from a magic-user is the fact that their magic and exotic possessions they have can be used against you! The charizard quickly found himself bound to the cavern he was in and constrained to a muzzle attached to a funnel leading upwards. Long before his belly could finish the candy he had stolen earlier, Charkol found the funnel attached to his face filling up with a thick, syrup-like concoction that had hints of the previous potions he had curiously downed earlier! 

Over the days and weeks after Halloween, Charkol had been fed a slow, but constant drip of the dense concoction as a fitting punishmnet for his earlier gluttony and thievery! Of course, the effects of the candy and enlarging potions had their effects with making Charkol quite large in ways that he wasn't exactly fond of!
