	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Normally, Charkol enjoys the luxury of being left alone, be it when he's traveling or while relaxing just outside of his home cavern. With the usual ease that this solitude grants, it came as a relative surprise that while he was far from his home, and in the midst of a very frustrating rut, a furred creature he didn't recognize had begun to tail and toy with him. The occasional rock or fruit thrown at his backside, teasing calls just to remind him that she was there and just the overall snickering drove him to the point of frustration. Of course, it didn't help that between his legs his unyielding arousal was egged on by his pent up balls, the pair feeling so heavy as they swung with every step he took. 

Eventually though, the mix of horniness and anger resulted in a fierce lashing out at whom he'd eventually learn to be Yevi. Amidst a flurry of fire and claws, Charkol tore down and incinerated the cover that the target of his ire was using to hide behind, leaving her within his grasp after a short while of persistent effort. Once he got his hands on her to at least deter future efforts to follow him, his mind was very much demanding that his sexual frustration be sated. Needless to say, Yevi was quite suited for the job!
