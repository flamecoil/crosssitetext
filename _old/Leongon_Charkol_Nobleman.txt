	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol was no stranger to failure, and was he no stranger to success, either. Having seized power in his municipality through blood, thievery and coercion, failure without some sort of believable reason and new course of action made his blood boil. Whenever he was doled petty excuses it wasn't hard to pick up on the charizard's tells that he was growing more and more impatient -- his brow furrowed, his posture would slouch, and occasionally he'd twitch and that fire would flare. 

Most who were familiar with the charizard knew very well to keep a close eye on the mechanical menace of an arm he had. Although the sounds were often quiet, the turning of gears, stretching of springs, and other hints of tension would reach ears that were listening for it. Most of his guards knew simply stand aside and let Charkol do what he wanted lest they risk being the subject of a burn or blade that was destined for the focus of his ire. 

When tension in his throne room was high one evening, Charkol offered up an opportunity of forgiveness after one of his newer hires failed to meet most of his expectations. "You're going to help relieve the stress you've caused me. You can either pick now, or I'll get up and decide for you."
