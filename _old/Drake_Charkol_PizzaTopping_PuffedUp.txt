	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Overeating, feasting and otherwise keeping his belly nice and taut quickly had Charkol realizing that there were very few around that could keep up with his pace of eating. Quantities of food rose along with his growing wastline and pudginess, but so did his awareness of others who were present that might have taken a similar delight in gorging themselves. Take out places often presented him with opportunities to get to know and become acquiantances with others who had a similarly greedy gut to him, resulting in either simple exchanges of favorite places to go or actually befriending them! 

A fellow charizard, Drake, was one to cross his path with quite a bit of enthusiasm, having no shyness about getting a bit close and encouraging Charkol to grow more comfortable with his newfound weight! With days and weeks going by, the two grew quite close, and while Charkol adopted Drake's feasting habits, the maroon male eased his friend into a couple of his lustful ones! Ultimately the two shared a lot of time snagging food and enjoying each other's company in a variety of ways!
