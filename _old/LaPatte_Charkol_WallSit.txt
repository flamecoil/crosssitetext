	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Living within a metropolitan area more often than not, Charkol puts his wings to work when life has him down, stressed, or simply in need for a moment to himself. Nooks and crannies of the city often hide genuinely nice spots that are rundown or low-traffic to watch the day go by to an album or few loaded up on his phone. As he lets his evening's soundtrack run, his mind slowly sorts itself out, at least for the short term. After a long evening of zoning out to music and the environment, Charkol is then able to take a nice long nighttime flight back home and sleep with little worrying him.
