	===== FURAFFINITY =====



After getting settled into lecturing for so many years, Charkol found himself picking up on mannerisms of his students much more easily, whether they were bored, engaged or otherwise. As lecturing got easier for him, he managed to put more time into studying these mannerisms; one school year a colorful dragon caught his eye mid-lecture, wearing a focused expression that he hadn't exactly seen before. Although he did get back to his lecture, he kept an eye on the dragon, Serulyian, in the coming weeks. Ultimately, Charkol figured out that the same look often came on days that he had walked into class a bit more primed for a bit of action than normal; from saucy text messages having been received before class to simply having not had a good fuck in a while. Thankfully, turning his attention to the eager dragon some made it quite easy to at least avoid the latter situation!
