	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol, as a massive glutton, is very much an unabashed creature of habit when it comes to his desire to devour whatever is within his reach and fits his tastes. Give him five minutes and any snack, appetizer, or meal can vanish down his gullet without very little trace of it ever existing. Despite his gut pretty much being the tell-tale sign of him being the most-likely reason for food disappearing without a trace, he'll mock you quite openly if you even ask him about what happened to your morsels!
