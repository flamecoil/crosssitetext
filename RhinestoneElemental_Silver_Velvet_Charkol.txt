	===== FURAFFINITY =====


When you manage to get your claws on a newly transformed human-pokemon hybrid, it's a little hard to not express some curiosity about them. Charkol and Velvet, their 'curiosity' lead them to both get a more thorough look at Silver. Their primary question was why she seemed to maintain the scent of a pokémon much more than the scent of a human when her appearance was the reverse -- and ultimately such an investigation lead to other things!
