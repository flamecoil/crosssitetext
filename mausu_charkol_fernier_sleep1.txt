	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Normally, Charkol is a pretty light sleeper, one to haul his rump out of bed when he is awoken by a noise in the night, even to the point in which he prefers to have some ambient noise about him to stay snoozing. Granted, when there are no audible disturbances, he sleeps quite soundly through the night, only to be woken up by a need conveyed by a growling, empty belly in the morning. While sprawled out on his bed, dreaming away of possibly hedonistic things, Charkol had a visitor in the night who was quite aware of his sleeping patterns. 

Fernier was quite aware and eager to take advantage of the charizard's hefty form, enough fat to easily serve as a make-shift bed, and enough muscle underneath it all to get him places at a pace that was surprising to most. Charkol's usual waking hunger was kept well at bay with the help of a full pizza, the gluttonous charizard quite able to even eat food in his sleep, albeit slowly. A little bit of innate magic behind some encouraging whispers ultimately put Charkol on his feet, much to Fernier's delight!
