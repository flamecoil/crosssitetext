Description
"Hey Charkol! Have you ever heard about Baketober?" Umbra cheerily asked of her new fire-type roommate. Charkol looked up quizzically from his laptop as Umbra tapped around on her tablet, smiling at what could only be assumed to be a plethora of proposed baking recipes put forth by some corner of the internet that was enthusiastic about baking. This was about roughly at the top of the month of October.

Umbra had pretty much become a baker without a shop to serve her goods at, the economic climate at the time making it neigh impossible to take on such a risky venture. Instead, she had the one 'customer' she needed to make sure that her drive to perfect one recipe a day was assessed by way of full consumption of each attempt. Despite lying to himself about keeping up the facade of putting on clothes that were far past the point of fitting and being structurally sound, Charkol did become a nuanced taste-tester for Umbra, each plate of pastries getting chomped down upon to in-turn offer up some insight into both his own personal tastes and what would possibly best serve her future attempts at the same recipe. 

Of course, both of them had fun with their respective role, but it became quite clear immediately that Charkol had to give up his role as re-stocker since getting to the grocery store by flight or by foot was pretty much out of the question as only a couple weeks had passed for his baking friend's challenge. Thankfully delivery services were quite able to keep up with Umbra's daily need for flour, eggs, and other baking goods to keep the charizard's belly stuffed!
