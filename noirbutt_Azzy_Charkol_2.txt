	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Charkol overall grew bigger and more aggressive as Azzy's supply of doughnuts shrank before the canine's very eyes. The fire-type's gut gurgled and churned as it was stuffed, working overtime to distribute the abundance of calories across the rest of the body with the assistance of the serums chugged earlier. By this point, Charkol was far used to managing extra weight on his frame, even relishing the feeling of a bloated gut and that only became bigger over time. 

When Azzy was ready to wrap up after the last sweet treat was devoured, Charkol was sure to make his intentions known: "Ah-ah...we're not done here just yet. You've got a smartphone, we're getting more doughnuts delivered, and I'm keepin' ya right here until they arrive." Needless to say, Azzy didn't particularly mind this arrangement!
