	===== FURAFFINITY =====


Prior to having signed up to be a test subject for some of the various serums that've changed his body, Charkol wasn't one to be okay with showing off his body in any way, shape or form. A low profile was something he preferred; attracting attention wasn't something he wanted to deal with on a frequent basis. So many years after his initial trial runs of just having to deal with some side-effects of one concoction or another, having to leave the house with a bulge that others can spot a mile away is no new occurrence. 

As such, Charkol over time got used to and somewhat enjoy the attention garnered in public, even going so far to try on outfits that he'd possibly never take a crack at in his more timid days. For the summer months, it didn't take long for a friend to convince him to snag a speedo and try it on, just to see if it could actually contain the hefty pride between his legs!
